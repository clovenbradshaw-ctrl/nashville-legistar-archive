 
AMENDMENT NO. 1 
TO CONTRACT NO.  321040 
BETWEEN 
METROPOLITAN GOVERNMENT OF NASHVILLE-DAVIDSON COUNTY 
AND 
D & D EVENTS, LLC 
This amendment is entered into, by and between the METROPOLITAN GOVERNMENT OF 
NASHVILLE-DAVIDSON COUNTY and  D & D EVENTS, LLC (hereinafter “Contractor”). 
WITNESSETH 
Whereas, the parties desire to change the terms and conditions of the contract agreement with 
and the Metropolitan Government of Nashville and Davidson County, the parties hereby agree to 
add of the following: 
The original contract holder, D & D EVENTS, LLC agrees to assign the contract to the newly 
named entity, D & D EVENTS, INC.   The new entity agrees to the terms and conditions of the 
original contract. 
Metro agrees to continue the contract under the new entity. 
This amendment shall not be binding upon the parties until it has been signed first by D & D 
EVENTS, LLC and D & D EVENTS, INC. then by the representatives of the Metropolitan 
Government and has been filed in the Office of the Metropolitan Clerk.  When it has been so 
signed and filed, this amendment shall be effective as of the date first written above.
