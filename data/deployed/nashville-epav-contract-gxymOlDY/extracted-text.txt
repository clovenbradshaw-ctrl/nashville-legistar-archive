THIS OPERATING AGREEMENT, made and entered into this day of
Movember 1 , 1989, by and between the METROPOLITAN
NASHVILLE AIRPORT AUTHORITY, a public corporation existing under
the laws of the State of Tennessee, hereinafter referred to as the
"Authority", and AMERICAN AIRLINES, hereinafter referred to as
"Airline".
WITNESSETH:

WHEREAS, the Authority is the owner and operator of the
Nashville International Airport, Nashville, Tennessee, together
with certain air navigational facilities; and

WHEREAS, at the request of the Airlines serving the Nashville
International Airport, the Authority has constructed a Triturator
Facility to support Airline Operations, and

WHEREAS, the Authority wishes to allow Airline to use the
Triturator Facility, as more fully described on Exhibits "A" and
"BM! attached hereto and made a part hereof.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants,
terms, conditions, privileges, obligations and agreements herein
contained, the Authority and Airline hereby mutually undertake,
promise and, agree, each for itself, and its successors and :
assigns, as follows: .

SECTION 1. TERM - the term of this Agreement shall be for a
period of one (1) year, commencing on the effective date of this
Agreement and shall be automatically renewed on a year-to-year
basis. Provided however, this Agreement may be terminated by
either party by giving written notice to the other party at least
thirty (30) days prior to any annual renewal date hereof.

SECTION 2. OPERATING RIGHTS - Airline is permitted to use,
in common with others authorized so to do, the Triturator
Facilities of Nashville International Airport for the disposal of
waste material required in connection with support activities for
a Scheduled Airline Business.

SECTION 3. OPERATING RESPONSIBILITIES OF AUTHORITY - The
Authority will manage and administer the Triturator Facilities in
support of Airline Operations at the Airport, and shall be .
responsible for the following:

1. Construction of facilities and installation of all

equipment. ,

2. Billing each Airline monthly a fee for debt service and
Operation & Maintenance Costs identified with the
operation of the Triturator Facility based upon the
Airline’s previous months total of enplaned passengers.

3. Maintaining the facilities in a clean, neat and sanitary
condition.

4. Repairing and maintaining the equipment and fixtures
associated with the ‘operation of the Triturator ~
Facilities.

5. Providing for sanitary trash removal.

6. Providing sweeping, snow removal and roadway maintenance
surrounding the facility.

SECTION 4. PRIVILEGES AND OBLIGATIONS OF AIRLINE - As on
Airline Tenant at the Nashville International Airport and a user
of the Triturator Facility, Airline will be responsible for the
following:

1. Pumping the waste material from Airline Equipment into

Triturator Facility.

SECTION 5. OPERATING FACILITIES - For the disposal of wastes,
Airline shall use the area on the Airport suitable for such
activities as shall be designated'by the Authority. The use of
such facilities shall be subject to such specific rules and
modifications as the Authority may require as the exigencies of
the immediate situation may dictate for the safe and proper
handling of vehicular traffic transporting such waste on the apron
area.’ ,

SECTION 6. USER FEES - Airline shall pay to the Authority as
compensation for construction and use of the Triturator Facility
the following: ;

1. A per enplanement fee established by the Authority to
cover the construction cost, debt service and operation
and Maintenance Costs identified with the operation of

the Triturator Facility, as agreed in the approved MIT
Ballot titled "Construct Triturator/Wash Rack"

These user fees may be adjusted from time to time to reflect _
as closely as possible, the actual debt service and Operation &
Maintenance Costs identified with the operation of the Triturator
Facility. ,

: SECTION 7. INDEMNIFICATIONS AND INSURANCE ~ Airline, under
the terms of this Agreement, will:not be in control or possession
of the Airport, and Airline does not assume responsibility for the
control or operation of the Airport or for the physical or other
conditions of same. It is expressly understood and agreed by and
between the Authorty and Airline that Airline is, and shall be, an
independent contractor and operator responsible to all parties for
all of its act or omissions and Authority shall not in any way be

Airline agrees to defend and to hold and save Authority
harmless from all liabilities and expenses, including expenses of
investigation and litigation in connection with any claims,
judgments, or other suits which may arise out of the alleged
negligence of Airline, its officers, employees or agents. In
addition, Airline shall, at its own expense, maintain proper and
sufficient primary liability insurance with a reputable insurance
company licensed to conduct business in the State of Tennessee.
The Authority shall be named as one of the insured, and shall be
furnished with a certificate of insurance, and such insurance shall , ;
not be subject to cancellation without a least thirty (30) days
advance written notice to Authority. Airline shall carry such
amounts of liability insurance as is generally regarded as
necessary or advisable in connection with the operation of the
business of Airline. In the event of any actual or alleged injury
or damage covered by the foregoing provisions of this paragraph it
shall be the responsibility of Airline, at no expense to Authority,
promptly to undertake any and all necessary investigative and legal
proceedings. The failure of Authority, at any time or from time
to time, to enforce the foregoing provisions of this paragraph
concerning insurance coverage shall not constitute a waiver of
those provisions nor in any respect reduce the obligation of _
Airline to defend and hold and save the Authority harmless with
respect to any items of injury or damage covered by this paragraph.

The Authority hereunder agrees to maintain in safe condition
all places in and about the Airport which are used by, or made
accessible to the public, such as waiting rooms, lavatories,
parking lots, streets, sidewalks and other approaches, etc., and
Authority further agrees to indemnify and hold harmless Airline
from any and all claims against the Airline arising out of and as
a result of the condition of any of the publicly used premises in
and about the Airport and Lessor further agrees to reimburse
Airline for any and all costs and expenses incurred in

may be hereafter adopted. Airline further agrees to abide by all
rules and regulations adopted by Authority relating to protection ~
of the environment.

fees. .

to the Agreement.
