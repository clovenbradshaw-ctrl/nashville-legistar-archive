GRANT CONTRACT 
BETWEEN THE METROPOLITAN GOVERNMENT 
OF NASHVILLE AND DAVIDSON COUNTY  
AND RED FROGS USA, INC 
This Grant Contract issued and entered into pursuant to Substitute Budget Ordinance BL2026-1377, by 
and between the Metropolitan Government of Nashville and Davidson County (“Metro”), and RED 
FROGS USA,INC, (“Recipient”), is for the provision of professional, trauma-informed services to victims 
of interpersonal violence further defined in the "SCOPE OF PROGRAM".   The Recipient’s annual report 
and audit are incorporated herein by reference. 
A. 
SCOPE OF PROGRAM:
A.1. 
The Recipient will use the funds to:
i.
Provide a supervised welfare and recovery point (Red Frogs' "Chill Out Zone") in the
Downtown Nashville Entertainment District during scheduled Friday and Saturday
evenings and major city events. The welfare and recovery point serves as a safe,
visible, non-enforcement location where individuals may receive hydration, phone
charging, welfare support, reunification assistance, safe transportation guidance, and
referrals to Metro Nashville Police Department, Nashville Fire Department EMS, or
other emergency services when needed.
ii.
The program also includes trained roaming volunteer teams deployed from the welfare
and recovery point to provide proactive welfare checks, harm minimization, safe walk
escorts, emotional support, de-escalation, visitor assistance, and early intervention
throughout the district. Services include volunteer recruitment, training, supervision,
and coordination with Metro public safety agencies and community partners to
enhance public safety, reduce preventable emergencies, and promote a safe and
welcoming downtown environment.
A.2. 
The Recipient must spend these funds consistent with the Grant Spending Plan, attached and
incorporated herein as Attachment 1.  The Recipient must collect data to evaluate the 
effectiveness of their services and must provide those results to Metro upon request.  
A.3. 
The Recipient will only utilize these funds for services the Recipient provides to documented
residents of Davidson County.  Documentation of residency may be established with a recent 
utility bill; voter's registration card; driver's license or other government issued-ID; current record 
from a school showing address; affidavit by landlord; or affidavit by a nonprofit treatment, shelter, 
half-way house, or homeless assistance entity located within Davidson County.  Recipient agrees 
that it will not use Metro funding for services to non-Davidson County residents. 
A.4. 
Additionally, the Recipient must collect data on the primary county of residence of the clients it
serves and provide that data to Metro upon request.

Red Frogs USA, Inc.
Statement of the Nature and Extent of the Organization's Program Serving Residents of the
Metropolitan Government
Metro Nashville Direct Appropriation FY27  |  Office of Family Safety  |  FEIN 92-0912235
Red Frogs USA is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization headquartered in Nashville, Tennessee. The
organization operates a trained volunteer harm reduction and personal safety program in the Lower
Broadway entertainment district of downtown Nashville on scheduled Friday and Saturday nights
throughout the year.
The program combines a supervised welfare and recovery point, located at 2nd Avenue and Broadway and
operating through the evening until the close of licensed trade, with roaming volunteer teams deployed
from that point into the surrounding district. Volunteers work on foot and in identifiable uniform. All services
are provided free of charge to any member of the public, without cost, registration, condition, or religious
requirement.
The welfare and recovery point gives the district something it otherwise lacks: a known, staffed,
non-enforcement location where a person who is intoxicated, separated from their group, or vulnerable can
be taken, can wait in safety, can be reunited with the people they came with, or can be handed to Metro
Nashville Police Department officers or Nashville Fire Department emergency medical personnel where a
situation exceeds the scope of volunteer response. Roaming teams identify people in difficulty and bring
them to that point.
Services include safe walk escorts for individuals who are intoxicated or separated from their group,
support for people who appear vulnerable to predatory behavior or who have become isolated from their
friends, drinking water, mobile phone charging so individuals retain the ability to contact rideshare, family,
or emergency services, and basic welfare support.
The program is non-enforcement and relationship-first by design. Volunteers are trained to engage without
judgment and to de-escalate, which allows them to reach individuals who would not otherwise approach
uniformed authorities. Red Frogs complements Metro's existing public safety infrastructure rather than
duplicating it, and reduces avoidable demand on police, emergency medical, and hospital resources.
Residents of the metropolitan government are served in several ways. Broadway's hospitality workforce,
including bar staff, servers, musicians, and venue security, are Davidson County residents who work in the
district every weekend and who regularly refer patrons of concern to Red Frogs volunteers. Local residents
using downtown for arena events, stadium events, festivals, and civic celebrations are served alongside all
other members of the public without distinction. The volunteer base itself is recruited and trained from the
greater Nashville community, providing a structured service pathway for Metro residents. All volunteers
complete background screening and Red Frogs training prior to deployment.
Metro funds appropriated to Red Frogs USA will be applied solely to the direct operation of this Nashville
program, as itemized in the accompanying budget and FY27 Program Spending Plan.

NOTE 1 - ORGANIZATION AND NATURE OF ACTIVITIES  
Red Frogs USA, Inc. (“Organization”) is a not-for-profit 501(c)(3) Tennessee corporation incorporated on 
November 18, 2022. Their programs are a direct response to their missions: To combat a culture in United States 
that is largely dominated by alcohol and other substances, often leading to dangerous and life-altering behaviors 
for young people. 
The Organization’s core programs include the following: 
• 
Red Frogs operates a weekly chill-out zone in Downtown Nashville on lower Broadway that operates 
from March - December. Each week Red Frogs engages and serves over 3,000 cups of water to 
individuals, assisting 50-100 people with walk homes, Ubers, way finding and connecting with lost 
friends. On top of that each week our teams respond to and assist on average 10 serious of high-risk 
situations preventing injury or harm to individuals by giving support and care. 
• 
Red Frogs support Nashville’s largest gatherings known as CMA Fest, July 4th “Let Freedom Sing”, and 
the NYE Big Bash. Red Frogs hands out over 100,000 cups of water and charges almost 5,000 phones 
providing safety strategies to the largest gatherings of people in Nashville.

(b) Basis of Presentation

Accounting Standards Codification (the FASB ASU 2016-14). In accordance with the Non-Profit

stipulations.

without donor restrictions and reported in the statement of activities as net assets released from

when a stipulated time restriction is accomplished, net assets with donor restrictions are reclassified as

restrictions.
