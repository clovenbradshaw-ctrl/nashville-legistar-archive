3. Rent and Rates. 
a. Rent:  The Licensee agrees to pay the Licensor the sum of $11,100.00 for the use of the 
premises. 
b. General Deposits: The total amount listed on the Invoice provided prior to the event is required as a 
non-refundable deposit upon signing this license agreement, and both the agreement & deposit 
must be tendered within 10 business days after the License Date. Payment will be by State of 
Tennessee warrant or direct deposit. The Board of Law Examiners is a tax-exempt Board of the 
Tennessee Supreme Court and will provide a Certificate of Exemption with this License 
Agreement. 
 
c. Clean Up- Fee Deposits. There is a refundable $1000.00 clean-up fee and damage deposit for all 
events held in The Fairgrounds Speedway and Grandstands, Fair Park and Brown’s Creek Lot. 
There is a refundable $500.00 clean-up and damage deposit per building rented. After 
inspection, if no clean-up is required and there are no damages to the premises, the deposit 
referenced in this subsection shall be returned. 
 
d. Move-In and Move-Out: The Licensor will allow the Licensee a ½ day Move-In (hereinafter 
“Move-In day”) beginning at noon on the day before the first day of the term of this License as 
listed in Section 2.a. and 2.b. of this agreement. It is understood by the Licensee that no other use 
of the premises other than set-up will be permitted on Move-In days. The Licensee agrees to pay 
to the Licensor “additional rent” equal to ½ day rental (hereinafter “Additional Rent”) if 
the Licensee moves in before noon on move in day, or if the Licensee sells, trades, opens 
to the public, displays, or swaps on Move-In day. Additional rent is also due for each ½ day 
that the Licensee occupies the premises over and above the Move-In day, the term of this License, 
or each ½ day the Licensee occupies the premises beyond midnight the last day of the term of this 
License. (See Section 12 on Vacation and Surrender of Premises). All rents shall be in the form of 
a bank cashier’s check.
