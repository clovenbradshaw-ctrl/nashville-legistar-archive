THIS MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING, made and entered into this _©& * day of
GULY > 1986, by and between the METROPOLITAN NASHVILLE AIRPORT
AUTHORITY, created pursuant to Tennessee Code Annotated, Section 42-4-101, et. .
seq., hereinafter referred to as the "Authority", and ELKINGTON & KELTNER
GROUP, INC., a Delaware corporation authorized to do business in the State of.
Tennessee, hereinafter referred to as -"E&K", The purpose of this Memorandum
of Understanding is to set forth the terms and conditions concerning E&k's
lease and development of certain real property located in the southeast
quadrant of Briley Parkway and Glastonbury Read in Davidson County, Tennessee,
containing 21.05 acres more or less, as more particularly depicted on Exhibit
: A and B, attached hereto and made a part hereof, hereinafter referred to as
the "Property". ;

1. In consideration of the posting of Earnest Money by E&K in the
amount of Ten Thousand Dollars ($10,000.00), within five (5) days .
after execution of this Memorandum of Understanding, the Authority

. agrees to undertake all actions and’ procedures necessary to obtain
all required Federal, State, and local governmental approvals,
signatory airline approvals, and other regulatory approvals required

. to be obtained by the Authority in order to allow the Authority to
lease and convey the Property. to E&K, by a Long Term Lease Agreement
(the “Lease"} upon the terms and conditions hereof. Said Earnest
Money shall be held in the Donelson, Stokes & Bartholomew, PAL,
_ Escrow Account.

2. The initial term of the Lease shall be fifty (50) years and there
shall be two (2) fifteen (15) year options to renew the Lease on the
terms and conditions contained therein. E&K shall give written

: notice of jits intention to ‘exercise each renewal option to the
Authority twelve (12) months prior to the end of the initial lease
term or renewal option and the Authority agrees to grant such a ;
renewal within six (6) months after such notice, provided the lessee
' is not in default under the Lease and the parties have agreed upon
the rental for the renewal lease term. The initial term of the


Lease shall commence upon the earlier of (i) the substantial comple-
tion of the construction of the proposed apartment units or (ii)
February 1, 1988 (the "Commencement Date").

3. The rental to be paid by E&K for the initial fifty (50) year term
shall be One Million Fifty-Two Thousand Five Hundred Dollars
($1,052,500.00) (the “Lease Principal", as such amount may be

‘ adjusted pursuant to the terms hereof). The Lease Principal shall
: be paid in full, in cash by E&K on the Commencement Date. The
annual rental to be paid by E&K during each fifteen (15) year option
, shall be equal to the fair market rental value of the Property based
on its highest and best use at the time each such option is
exercised (adjusted every 24 months) as determined by an appraisal
on the same general terms as set forth in Paragraph 4 hereof. It is
contemplated that rental for such extension periods shall be payable
; on an annual or monthly basis and not on a lump-sum basis for such
extension periods.

4. E&K shall provide, at its sole cost and expense, an appraisal of the
Property conducted by an MAI or SREA qualified appraiser acceptable
to the Authority. The appraisal shall state the fair market value
of the Property and the Lease Principal shall be adjusted upward,
but not downward, based upon such appraisal, so that the Lease
Principal equals the greater of the appraised value of the Property
or $1,052,500. Provided, if the appraised value js greater than

_ $1,052,500, E&K shall have ten (10) days to accept or reject such
value and in the event E&K accepts such value, the parties will
Proceed under this agreement and in the event E&K rejects such value
this agreement shall be terminated and the parties shall have no
further obligation or liability one to the other.

5. Within thirty (30) days after execution of this Memorandum of
Understanding, the Authority, at its sole cost and expense, shall
Provide E&K with an interim title insurance binder which reflects
the state of the title to the Property, the same to be updated to
the date of Closing. Said binder shall require the issuance of a

leasehold title insurance policy to E&K at Closing, subject only to
Permitted Exceptions (as hereinafter defined). E&K shall have
fifteen (15) days from receipt of said binder to advise the
Authority of any objections to the title affecting its market-
ability. The Authority shall thereafter have sixty (60) days to
satisfy or make provisions for the satisfaction of such objections,
but the Authority shall be under no obligation to do so. If the
Authority fails, is unable or chooses not to cure such objections
within the time specified, E&K may, upon written notice to the
Authority within ten (10) days of the expiration of such sixty (60)
day period, execute and close the Lease subject to such matters
("Permitted Exceptions"), without reduction in the Lease Principal,
or in the alternative may cancel this Memorandum of Understanding
and the Earnest Money (exclusive of any additional Earnest Money)
shall be returned to E&K and the parties shall have no further
obligations or liability one unto the other.

. 6. The Authority shal] provide E&K a certified boundary survey of the
Property within thirty (30) days after the execution of this
Memorandum of Understanding. E&K shall have fifteen (15) days after
completion of the survey to notify the Authority of any materially
adverse conditions or matters affecting the Property reflected in
the survey. The Authority shall thereafter have sixty (60) days to
remove or make provision for the removal of such matters, but shal]
be under no obligation to do so. If the Authority fails, is unable
or chooses not to remove such matters within the time specified, E&K
may, upon written notice to the Authority within ten (10) days of
the expiration of such sixty (60) day period, execute and close the
Lease subject to such matters without reduction in the Lease Princi-
pal, or, alternatively, cancel this Memorandum of Understanding and
the Earnest Money (exclusive of any additional Earnest Money) shall
thereupon be returned to E&K and the parties shall have no further
obligation or liability one unto the other. The cost of the survey
shall be shared equally by E&K and the Authority, regardless of
whether the Lease is executed -and closed. .

7. Interest on the Lease Principal, as adjusted, shall be paid by E&K
to the Authority from the date of Closing through the Commencement
. Date as follows:
Interest on the Lease Principal at a rate per annum equal to
one percent (1%) above the prime lending rate published in the
' Wall Street Journal for U.S. Money Center Commercial Banks, as
. , such rate may change from time to time, shall accrue beginning
on the date of Closing, and payments of all accrued interest to
date shall be due and payable every ninety (90) days thereafter
through the Commencement Date. The interest rate shall be
established as of the Closing and shall be adjusted every
thirty (30) days thereafter. A11 accrued and unpaid interest
shall be due “and payable with the Lease Principal on the
Commencement Date. : :

the following conditions precedent:
(a) Approval of the final site plan for E&K's proposed development
of the Property by the Metropolitan Government of Nashville and
Davidson County, Tennessee, permitting at least 294 apartment
units.
(b) Availability of utilities with adequate capacity to service the
. . proposed apartment development. .
(c)’ Reasonable access to Briley Parkway.
, (d) Negotiation of the Lease on the terms and conditions set forth
herein, with such additional terms as are reasonably satisfac-
tory to E&k.
Upon the execution of this Memorandum of Understanding, E&K shall
begin and shall diligently pursue satisfaction of the aforesaid
contingencies. : .

 gencies.

aircraft in the area of the Property and the noise associated
