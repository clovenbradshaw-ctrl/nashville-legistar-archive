MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING 
 
This MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING (MOU) entered this ___ day of _____, 2025, between the 
Nashville Department of Transportation (hereafter referred to as NDOT) and The Fairgrounds Nashville (hereafter 
referred to as Fairgrounds). 
 
WITNESSETH 
 
WHEREAS, each of the entities participating in this MOU are departments/branches of The Metropolitan Government 
of Nashville and Davidson County; and, 
 
WHEREAS, NDOT is currently engaged in a project to build a sidewalk on Craighead Avenue; and, 
 
WHEREAS, as a result of the construction of the new sidewalk, stormwater runoff will be directed onto Fairgrounds 
property; and,  
 
WHEREAS, it is mutually beneficial to establish a framework for the management of stormwater runoff along 
Craighead Avenue adjacent to the Fair Park Dog Park; 
 
NOW, THEREFORE, IN CONSIDERATION OF THE PROMISES AND COVENANTS AND AGREEMENTS 
CONTAINED HEREIN, THE PARTIES HEREBY AGREE AS FOLLOWS: 
 
1. Compensatory Cut Volume 
Fairgrounds will provide a compensatory cut volume for borrow fill material within the 100-year 
floodplain in the amount of 255.2 cubic yards. NDOT will place the borrow fill material in the 
appropriate area to manage stormwater runoff. 
 
2. Stormwater Collection and Discharge: 
NDOT will install a French drain system and outfall piping along Craighead Avenue as part of the 
Craighead Sidewalk Phase 4 project to collect stormwater runoff. The collected runoff will be 
discharged into the permeable paving parking lot owned by Fairgrounds. 
 
3. Design and Installation: 
NDOT will be responsible for the design and installation of the French drain system and outfall piping. 
All installations will comply with relevant local, state, and federal regulations regarding stormwater 
management. 
 
4. Maintenance and Monitoring: 
Fairgrounds will maintain the French drain system, outfall piping, and permeable paving parking lot to 
ensure its effectiveness in stormwater infiltration. Fairgrounds will also monitor the parking lot to 
ensure it continues to function as intended for stormwater management. 
 
5. Funding: 
The funding for the installation of the French drain system and accompanying outfall piping will be 
provided through the budget allocated for the Craighead Sidewalk Phase 4 project. Ongoing 
maintenance of the permeable paving parking lot, French drain system, and outfall piping will be 
funded by Fairgrounds. 
 
6. Duration and Review: 
This MOU shall remain in effect from the date of signing and will be subject to periodic reviews and 
updates to ensure their ongoing relevance and accuracy.

7. Termination and Amendments: 
Either party may terminate this agreement within 90 days of providing written notice. Any amendments 
to this MOU shall require the mutual and written consent of both NDOT and Fairgrounds. 
 
8. Governing Law: 
This MOU shall be governed by the laws of Tennessee.  
 
This MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING sets forth the entire agreement between the parties with respect to 
the subject matter hereof and shall govern the respective duties and obligations of the parties.
