THE NASHVILLE FAIR 2023 
ENTERTAINMENT CONTRACT 
CONTRACT # 007 
 
 
 
This Contract (“Contract” or “Agreement”) is entered into this 29th day of July, 2023, by 
and between the Metropolitan Government of Nashville and Davidson County, Tennessee, acting 
by and through the authority delegated to The Nashville Fair by the Metropolitan Board of Fair 
Commissioners (collectively, “METRO”), and Joseph Thomas an Individual (state of formation 
and entity type, or individual), d/b/a/ The Mystical Misfits (if applicable), with a business 
address of 952 General George Patton Road, Nashville, TN 37221 (“EVENT”). 
 
METRO operates The Nashville Fair, which shall be held on September 8-17, 2023 on 
the premises of The Fairgrounds Nashville (“Fair Premises”).  EVENT provides entertainment as 
described herein and enters into this Contract in order to provide such entertainment at The 
Nashville Fair (“Performance”).  In consideration of the mutual covenants, terms and conditions 
set forth herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of 
which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows: 
 
1. 
Services. 
 
• The EVENT agrees to provide the entertainment described in the attached rider, 
Exhibit A. 
• The EVENT also agrees to provide the additional labor, equipment, and other 
necessary items and services as described in the attached rider, Exhibit A. 
• METRO agrees to provide the facilities, equipment, and other items as described 
in the attached rider, Exhibit A. 
 
2. 
Term.  
The Contract shall become effective upon the Contract being fully 
executed by all Parties.  The Term of the Contract and shall end on September 17, 2023, the last 
day of The Nashville Fair.   
3. 
 Compensation. 
METRO agrees to pay $1,000.00 to EVENT for the entertainment 
provided in accordance with the terms and conditions of this Contract.  This compensation shall 
not exceed $2,000.00.   
EVENT shall be paid after their performance, upon determination by METRO that EVENT has 
satisfactorily provided the agreed upon EVENT services.  There will be no other charges or fees

4. 
Termination.  
Should EVENT fail to fulfill its obligations under the Contract in a 
timely and proper manner, or if it should violate any term of the Contract, METRO shall have 
the right to immediately terminate the Contract.  Such termination shall not relieve EVENT of 
any liability to METRO for damages sustained by virtue of any breach by EVENT. 
 
5. 
Recording and Broadcast Rights. EVENT agrees to allow METRO to photograph, 
film and/or record EVENT’s activities at The Nashville Fair and permits the limited use of such 
photographs or recordings, including publication, reproduction, and distribution, by METRO 
solely for promotional and fundraising purposes associated with The Nashville Fair.   
 
6. 
Parking.  METRO shall provide two (2) parking spaces for EVENT, which shall be in 
designated parking spaces on the Fair Premises.  METRO shall not be responsible for any loss or 
damage occurring to vehicles brought onto Fair Premises by EVENT, its employees and 
subcontractors.  
 
7. 
Access. 
EVENT shall have right of access to the premises as permitted by 
METRO, as well as access to the Fair Premises as necessary for the purposes of installing and 
removing necessary goods and equipment.  EVENT, its subcontractors, and employees shall 
have access to METRO premises upon the presentation of passes to be issued by METRO.  
METRO reserves the right to exclude or eject all objectionable persons from the Fair Premises 
without liability. 
 
8. 
Control of Premises.  METRO maintains the right to control the management and 
operation of the Fair Premises and enforce the necessary and proper rules of METRO and The 
Nashville Fair at all times.   
 
9. 
Condition and Maintenance of Facility.  EVENT shall maintain the Fair Premises that 
it occupies during the course of its Performance in a safe and orderly manner and cause all debris 
to be properly discarded.  EVENT shall immediately pay METRO for the cost of repairing any 
damage to the Fair Premises caused by the acts, omissions, or negligence of EVENT, its 
employees and contractors, or its invitees during the term of this Contract.   
 
10. 
Responsibility for Property. 
METRO assumes no responsibility for any property 
placed or left on the Fair Premises by EVENT and is hereby expressly relieved and discharged 
from any and all liability for any loss, injury, or damage, to persons or property that may be 
sustained by reason of use of the Fair Premises, unless it is proved that such loss, injury, or 
damage is caused by METRO’s own negligent acts. 
 
11. 
Lost or Abandoned Property.  METRO shall have the right to collect and maintain 
custody of all items left on the Fair Premises after the expiration of the term of the Contract.  
Any property left on the Fair Premises after that point shall be deemed abandoned by EVENT 
and may be disposed of by METRO as its sole discretion.  EVENT shall be responsible for any 
costs involved with the storage, disposal, or removal of its abandoned property.
