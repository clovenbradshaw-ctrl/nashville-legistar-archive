This Grant Contract, by and between the Metropolitan Government of NashviJle and Davidson County, Tennessee, by and through the 
Metropolitan Nashville Arts Commission, hereinafter referTed to as the "Grantor" and PENCIL Foundation, hereinafter referTed to 
as the "Grantee," is for application number 16AS09 for the provision of a/an After-School Arts Project Support grant, as 
further defined in the "SCOPE OF SERVICES." 
The Grantee is a nonprofit charitable or civic organization. The Grantee's address is listed below in E.2. The Grantee's application is 
on file with the Grantor in the Grantee's file for application number 16AS09. 
The Grantee's place of incorporation or organization is the State of Tennessee. 
A.
SCOPE OF SER VICES:
A.1.
The Grantee shall employ the funds made available under this grant in accordance with the project/program submitted in the
application for a/an After-School Arts Project Support grant, which application is incorporated herein, and subject to the Arts
Commission's FY2016 Grant Guidelines and to the tenns and conditions set forth herein.
A.2.
The Grantee, under this Grant Contract, will spend funds solely for the purpose set forth in application number 16AS09.
B.
GRANT CONTRACT TERM:
B. I. 
Grant Contract Term. This Grant Contract shall be effective for the period commencing on July 1, 2015 and ending on June
I, 2016. The Grantor shall have no obligation for services rendered by the Grantee which are not performed within the
specified period.
C.
PAYMENT TERMS AND CONDITIONS:
C. I.
Maximum Liability. In no event shall the maximum liability of the Grantor under this Grant Contract exceed $2,305. The
Grantee agrees to match with cash the Grantor's compensation under this grant with a minimum sum of$2,305 as identified
in application number I 6AS09. The Grantee shall use the award and match in the manner provided on the approved Revised
Budget, attached and incorporated herein as a part of this Grant Contract as Attachment One (I). The Revised Budget line­
items include, but are not limited to, all applicable taxes, fees, overhead, and all other direct and indirect costs incurTed or to
be incurred by the Grantee.
C.2.
Compensation Firm. The maximum liability of the Grantor under this Grant Contract is firm for the duration of the Grant
Contract and is not subject to escalation for any reason unless amended pursuant to Section 0.2 of this Grant Contract.
C.3.
Payment Methodology. Upon execution of the Grant Contract and receipt of a request for payment, the Grantee may be
eligible to receive up to eighty percent (80%) of the grant amount. The final payment, or remaining twenty percent (20%) of 
the grant amount, will be made only after the Grantee has completed the project/program and properly documented all
monies spent (including the grantee's match) toward the project/program. The Grantee shall expend these grant funds and
the required match before requesting additional grant funds, in the manner described in C.3.a.
C.3.a. 
The final Grant Contract payment shall be made only after the Grantee has completed the project/program and has submitted
a final financial report of expenditures documenting a full accounting of the expenditures made in accomplishing this Grant 
Contract, including its own cash match, identification and amounts of in-kind contributed services (if any). The final financial 
report shall be completed in accordance with the Revised Budget on tile with the Grantor in the Grantee's file and shall be in 
form and substance acceptable to the Grantor. 
C.3.b. 
The final payment request and financial report of expenditures are to be submitted by the Grantee within thirty (30) days after
the ending date of the project/program, or by June I, 2016, whichever comes first. Project grantees must submit the Closeout 
Report with their final payment request. Basic Operating Support grantees must submit the Closeout Report no later than July 
31, 2016.
