DEMOLITION SCOPE OF WORK - CODES 
1. Preparation for Demolition Services
Permits 
All demolition performed within Metro requires a permit from Metro Department of Codes 
Administration. All necessary permits shall be obtained by the Contractor prior to the start of all 
demolition activities. Additionally, each application for a permit shall include a proposed plan for the 
disposal of C&D waste. Such plan shall include a statement, signed by the Contractor, providing that no 
construction and demolition waste will be stored on the property or disposed of in a non-approved 
dumpsite. 
Prior to demolition, Contractor will contact TN ONE Call and coordinate with local utility service 
providers to assure that services are properly disconnected, closed and shut off. This includes 
water/sewer, electrical, telephone, cable, internet, and natural gas. 
Water/Sewer  
Water lines and sewer laterals serving the demolished structure(s) shall remain in place and on site for 
subsequent capping at the associated main lines by Metro. 
Electricity  
Prior to demolition, all electrical meter boxes and utility lines should have been removed by the service 
provider. Contractor shall notify Metro if any utility connections to the residential structure remain. 
Telephone/Cable/Internet  
Prior to demolition, all telephone, cable, and internet service wires should be rolled back to the main 
utility poll by the service provider. Contractor shall notify Metro if any utility connections to the 
residential structure remain.  
Natural Gas  
Prior to demolition, all natural gas meters and utility lines should have been removed by the service 
provider. Contractor shall notify Metro if any utility connections to the residential structure remain.

- 
If discovery of an underground fuel storage tank takes place during demolition, Contractor shall

by Metro, not Contractor. Upon removal, Contractor shall backfill the void as stated above. 
 
Fill/Compaction of Small Voids (less than 2 feet deep) Contractor shall fill voids, depressions or holes 
that were created as a result of the demolition activities with topsoil or clean fill dirt. The demolition site 
shall be graded to drain and free of protrusions, demolition debris and abrupt edges. The goal is to 
create the desired topography, soil profiles and drainage patterns to support restoration efforts and to 
assure that the existing storm water system can be maintained after completion of the project. 
 
Final Restoration 
Overgrowth shall be removed from the areas of demolition prior to final restoration. Final grading must 
include amending existing site soil and/or fill dirt by incorporating at least 2-inches of composted soil 
organic matter tilled into 4-inches of existing site soil and/or fill dirt, raked level, and surface rocks larger 
than one inch removed prior to seeding. Final restoration shall consist of seeding with a cool season 
grass mixture such as Kentucky bluegrass and site stabilization using a vegetative cover such as wheat 
straw or hay. The final restored area of demolition shall be free of voids and pockets and in a condition 
that is suitable for mowing with a push type mower. An inspection is required from Codes personnel 
prior to invoicing. Demolition Contractor will contact Codes as properties are completed and restored. 
 
All streets and sidewalks shall be protected during demolition and swept clean upon completion of the  
project. Any damage to these as a result of this contract will be repaired/replaced to NDOT standards at 
no cost to Metro.
