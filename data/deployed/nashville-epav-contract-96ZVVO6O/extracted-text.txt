whatsoever and at any time in any part of the world.

shall have the right to use the SITE at a later date to be mutually agreed upon by the parties, but METRO has no 
obligation to delay changes to or other use of the SITE pending such use. 
4. 
FILM MAKER shall provide crowd control, noise control, and security. Should METRO deem FILM MAKER's 
provisions for same to be inadequate, FILM MAKER shall provide crowd control, noise control, and security at 
the level required by METRO.

condition and as clean and orderly as when found by FILM MAKER.
