3. 
Premises Condition. The Premises shall be in a good state of repair and cleanliness at Move-In. Licensee 
has inspected the Premises and determined them to be suitable in all respects for conducting the Event. 
Based on its inspection, Licensee has determined what electrical, other utility services, equipment and 
facilities are available on the Premises and will arrange and pay for the delivery of electrical and other 
utility services and provide other equipment and facilities necessary for the Event but not currently 
available. Licensee shall ensure that existing bathrooms are in good working order for access by 
event artists only. 
4. 
Portable Restrooms. The Licensee is responsible for providing sufficient portable restrooms and hand 
washing stations or sanitizing stations to accommodate the projected attendance and must include ADA 
portable restrooms. 
5. 
Use by Licensee. Licensee shall have the right to use the Premises only in accordance with the terms of 
this license agreement. 
Licensee shall not use any part of the Fairgrounds not comprising part of the 
Premises. Licensee shall have the right to charge admission to the Event and the retain the revenues 
associated therewith. Licensee may use temporary signage on the Premises consistent with Licensor's 
rules and regulations. Licensee may enter into sponsorship agreements pertaining to the Event and retain 
the revenues associated therewith.

execution of this agreement and $15,000 (the "Balance") prior to Move-In. Payments due under 
this agreement shall be made by cashier's check, certified check, wire transfer or other form

Protection of Fairgrounds Property.

Licensee to its condition prior to the Event unless caused by Licensor or parties for whom 
Licensor is responsible. Without limiting the foregoing, unless caused by Licensor or parties 
for whom Licensor is responsible, Licensee shall be responsible for restoring all plants and 
turf damaged during the Event, whether due to the movement of large equipment, garbage 
dumpsters, stages, crowds, other vehicles and equipment, or other causes.

brought onto the Premises by Licensee prior to Move-Out. Licensee shall pay Licensor 
upon demand all costs contemplated by this section to the extent such costs exceed the 
deposit referenced in subsection 3(b) of this agreement. Licensee shall not be responsible 
for damage to the Premises caused by Licensor or its agents, officers or employees. 
c. 
Licensee may not satisfY its obligations under this section by assignment of insurance benefits.

9. Security. Licensee agrees to furnish a security plan to Licensor at least 30 days prior to the Event. The 
security plan must include a sufficient number of security officers and/or police officers to maintain law 
and order. Licensor shall have the right to require changes to the security plan to comport with best

10. Vacation of Premises and Surrender. Licensee shall vacate and peaceably surrender the Premises to 
Licensor not later than Move-Out. If Licensee fails to do so, then Licensor may remove from the 
Premises all items remaining thereon and store or dispose of them as Licensor may deem fit at 
Licensee's sole cost, expense, and risk. Licensor shall not be liable to Licensee for removing and storing 
such property. 
11. Cancellation. Licensee may cancel this agreement by delivering written notice to Licensor at least 30 
days prior to Move-ln. If Licensee cancels as provided in this section, Licensor shall be entitled to retain 
the Deposit as liquidated damages. Licensee shall pay Licensor the full rental provided in section 5 of this 
agreement if Licensee cancels later than 30 days prior to Move-ln. 
12. Concessions. Licensor reserves for itself its Concessionaire the sole right to sell and serve all food and 
beverages of every kind. Licensor will initiate and facilitate contact between the Licensee and 
Concessionaire for Event-planning purposes. If the Concessionaire is unable or unwilling to 
provide service based on reasonable request as determined by Licensor, an alternative vendor, 
properly licensed as required by Applicable Law, may be engaged upon written authorization by 
Licensor. Licensee shall use reasonable efforts not locate or permit the location of any Event 
structure or signage within 1 0 feet of a concession booth operated by Concessionaire. 
13. Like Shows. Significantly Similar Shows: It is the desire of The Fairgrounds to have a 
complimentary mix of entertainment events for the mutual benefit of the public and the promoters. 
Events that are of a significantly similar nature may affect attendance and/or appeal to the public. 
Therefore, The Fairgrounds reserves the right to require a reasonable period of time between 
scheduling events that are significantly similar in nature. 
14. Parking. Licensor shall be entitled to charge for parking for the Event. Licensor will remit to Licensee 
$5.00 for each parking permit sold prior to the Event and $7.00 for each parking permit sold during the 
Event. Such payment shall be remitted within 30 business days at the end of the event. 
15. Licenses and Taxes. Licensee shall obtain at its sole cost all licenses necessary for the Event to comply

16. Control of Premises. Licensor shall have the right to enter the Premises at all times for the purposes of 
protecting the Premises from harm and enforcing this agreement and the rules of operation governing use 
of the Premises. 
17. Use of Premises By Licensor. Licensor may use any part of the Premises at any time not in actual conflict 
with use or previously planned use by Licensee. 
18. Hazardous Substances. Licensee shall not bring upon the Premises any substance, exhibit, equipment or 
vehicles which, in the sole reasonable judgment of the Executive Director, would be or might be 
dangerous to persons or property or otherwise incompatible with the structure, systems, and furnishings of 
the Premises or constitute a violation of Applicable Law. 
19. Termination. Should either party fail to fully comply with its obligations under this agreement, the 
other party shall have the right to terminate the agreement after delivery of written notice to the 
breaching party and a provision of reasonable opportunity to cure the breach. In the event of a breach by 
Licensee, Licensee shall be liable to Licensor for all damages sustained by Licensor on account of such 
breach. In the event of a breach by Licensor, Licensee shall be entitled to all damages sustained by 
Licensee on account of such breach, not to exceed the sum actually paid by Licensee to Licensor pursuant 
to the terms this agreement at the time of such breach. No waiver by either party of any default shall 
operate as a waiver of any other default, or the same default on a future occasion. No delay or omission 
by either party in exercising any right or remedy shall operate as a waiver thereof, and no single or 
partial exercise of a right or remedy shall prelude any other further exercise thereof, or the exercise of any 
other right or remedy. 
20. Modification. No modification of this agreement shall be valid unless in writing and signed by authorized 
representatives of both parties. 
21. No Partnership or Joint Venture. Nothing in this agreement shall be construed to create a partnership or 
joint venture between Licensor and Licensee.

property protected by copyright will be performed, reproduced or used, nor will the name of any entity

obtained written permission from the trademark or copyright holder. Licensee covenants to comply 
strictly with all Applicable Law respecting copyright, royalties, and trademarks and warrants that it will 
not infringe any related right of any person during its use of the Premises. Licensee will indemnify and 
hold the Indemnified Parties harmless from all claims, losses and damages (including court costs and

Licensee may not assign its rights or responsibilities under this agreement without 
Licensor's express written consent. 
27. Governing Law and Venue. Tennessee law shall govern any dispute arising under this agreement. Any 
action arising under this agreement shall be brought exclusively in the Circuit or Chancery Courts for 
Davidson County, Tennessee. 
28. Force Majeure. If the Premises is destroyed or damaged by fire or any other cause, or if any other 
casualty or unforeseen occurrence (any such event being a "Force Majeure Event") renders the fulfillment 
of this License by Licensor impossible, this agreement shall terminate, and Licensee shall be liable only 
for rent which has accrued at the time of such Force Majeure Event. Licensee hereby waives any claim 
for damages or compensation from Licensor on account of such termination. 
29. Severability. The declaration by a court of competent jurisdiction that a provision of this agreement is 
invalid shall not affect the enforcement of any other part of this agreement. 
30. Merger. This agreement contains the entire understanding of the parties with respect to the subject matter 
hereof.
