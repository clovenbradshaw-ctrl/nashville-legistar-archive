AMENDMENT NUMBER 4 TO 
METROPOLITAN BOARD OF PUBLIC EDUCATION CONTRACT  
WITH  
OASIS CENTER

PURCHASE OF SERVICES 
 
This amendment is entered into by and between the Metropolitan Government of Nashville 
and Davidson County by and through the Metropolitan Board of Public Education (“MBPE” 
or “MNPS”) and Oasis Center, 1704 Charlotte Avenue, Suite 200, Nashville, TN 37203 
(“Contractor”). 
 
 
W I T N E S S E T H 
 
WHEREAS, the parties desire to modify the terms and conditions and to add or delete 
certain other terms and conditions to their original agreement filed with the Metro Clerk’s Office 
on March 12, 2018, MBPE Contract 2-173773-01, including Amendments 1-3, collectively 
hereinafter the “Contract”, the parties hereby agree as set forth below. 
 
1. The number assigned to the Contract is hereby changed from 2-173773-01 to 10301.  Any 
reference to the Contract number moving forward shall be 10301. 
 
2. Attachment F: Budget for 2019-2020 is hereby removed and replaced with Attachment F 
(Rev1), attached hereto. 
3. Section 1: Duties and Responsibilities of Contractor of the Contract has hereby been 
modified to read as follows: 
1. 
Duties and Responsibilities of Contractor.  Contractor agrees to provide and MNPS agrees to purchase the Title I 
tutoring and instructional materials, equipment and therapies for students at the Oasis Center facility according to the 
details outlined in Attachment F (Rev1) and Attachment G. 
Services provided under this Contract will be ordered and supplied on an as needed basis only.  
Nothing in this Contract shall be construed as a minimum guarantee of services to be ordered from Contractor. 
4. Section 5 a) of the Contract has hereby been modified to read as follows: 
a) 
Contractor shall to be paid according to details outlined in Attachment F (Rev1) and the billing distribution outlined below: 
i) 
Instructional Cost- Instructional materials, supplies, software, equipment and therapies to increase the capacity 
of the Title I eligible students 
ii) 
Professional Development Cost- Materials, supplies, software, equipment; services for teachers of Title I 
students employed by the participating school/facility 
iii) 
Parental involvement- Materials, supplies, software, therapies; services for students of Title I students who are 
housed at the participating facilities 
5. The total compensation in Section 5 d) of the Contract has been increased by $513.05 to a 
new not to exceed total of $94,174.07.
