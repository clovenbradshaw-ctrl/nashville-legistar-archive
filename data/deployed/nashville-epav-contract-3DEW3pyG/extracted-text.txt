GRANT CONTRACT 
BETWEEN THE  
THE METROPOLITAN GOVERNMENT  
OF NASHVILLE AND DAVIDSON COUNTY, TENNESSEE 
BY AND THROUGH 
THE METROPOLITAN NASHVILLE ARTS COMMISSION 
AND  
Actors Bridge Ensemble Theater of Nashville Inc 
 
This Grant Contract, by and between the Metropolitan Government of Nashville and Davidson County, Tennessee, by and through the 
Metropolitan Nashville Arts Commission, hereinafter referred to as the “Grantor” and Actors Bridge Ensemble Theater of 
Nashville Inc, hereinafter referred to as the “Grantee,” is for contract B2101 for the provision of a Actors Bridge Ensemble 
Theater of Nashville Inc grant, as further defined in the “SCOPE OF SERVICES.” 
 
The Grantee is a nonprofit charitable or civic organization. The Grantee's address is listed below in E.2. The Grantee’s application is 
on file with the Grantor in the Grantee's file for contract B2101.  
 
 The Grantee's place of incorporation or organization is the State of Tennessee. 
 
A. 
SCOPE OF SERVICES: 
 
A.1. 
The Grantee shall employ the funds made available under this grant in accordance with the program submitted in the 
application for a Actors Bridge Ensemble Theater of Nashville Inc grant, which application is incorporated herein, and 
subject to the Arts Commission’s Grant Guidelines and to the terms and conditions set forth herein. 
 
A.2. 
The Grantee, under this Grant Contract, will spend funds solely for the purpose set forth in contract number B2101. 
 
B. 
GRANT CONTRACT TERM: 
 
B.1. 
Grant Contract Term 1.  This Grant Contract shall be effective for the period commencing on July 1, 2020 and ending on 
June 1, 2021 for Basic Operating Support, Core Operating Support, Creation Project Support, and Catalyst Project Support. 
The Grantor shall have no obligation for services rendered by the Grantee which are not performed within the specified 
period. 
 
B.2. 
Grant Contract Term 2. For Arts Access Project Support and Youth Arts Project Support, this Grant Contract shall be 
effective for the period commencing on July 1, 2020 – June 15, 2021. The Grantor shall have no obligation for services 
rendered by the Grantee which are not performed within the specified period. 
 
C. 
PAYMENT TERMS AND CONDITIONS: 
 
C.1. 
Maximum Liability.  In no event shall the maximum liability of the Grantor under this Grant Contract exceed $19400 
(Nineteen Thousand Four Hundred  Dollars and No Cents).  The Grantee agrees to match with cash the Grantor's 
compensation under this grant with a minimum sum of $19400 (Nineteen Thousand Four Hundred  Dollars and No Cents) as 
identified in contract number B2101. The Grantee shall use the award and match in the manner provided on the approved 
Revised Budget, attached and incorporated herein as a part of this Grant Contract as Attachment One (1). The Revised 
Budget line-items include, but are not limited to, all applicable taxes, fees, overhead, and all other direct and indirect costs 
incurred or to be incurred by the Grantee. 
 
C.2. 
Compensation Firm.  The maximum liability of the Grantor under this Grant Contract is firm for the duration of the Grant 
Contract and is not subject to escalation for any reason unless amended pursuant to Section D.2 of this Grant Contract. 
 
C.3. 
Payment Methodology.  Upon execution of the Grant Contract and receipt of a request for payment, the Grantee may be 
eligible to receive up to eighty percent (80%) of the grant amount.  The final payment shall equal the total of the Maximum 
Liability described in C.1. minus the amount received upon execution  or remaining twenty percent (20%) of the grant 
amount whichever is greater. The final payment will be made only after the Grantee has completed the program and properly 
documented all monies spent (including the grantee's match) and submitted detailed project outcomes as requested in the 
Final Closeout Report.  The Grantee shall expend these grant funds and the required match before requesting additional grant 
funds, in the manner described in C.3.a.
