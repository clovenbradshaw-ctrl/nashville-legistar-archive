Scope of Services

mobility. 
•

equipment used to sell or display beverages, at no cost to Fairgrounds. 
•

shall be the responsibility of the Contractor to repair any damage to the facilities and/or facilities’ 
equipment caused by the Contractor’s operations or negligence. 
• 
Contractor will maintain all areas provided by the Fairgrounds and used by the Contractor in 
performing this contract in a clean and safe condition. 
• 
Equipment and other items owned by or under the control of the Contractor for use on-site shall 
always be stored in places approved by the Fairgrounds and in compliance with any and all 
applicable laws and regulations of the Metropolitan Government of Nashville and Davidson County. 
• 
Contractor will submit in writing to the Fairgrounds or his/her designee requests for the relocation, 
addition, or removal of vending machine equipment, prior to any such changes being made.

• 
Contractor will require its employees to always wear nametags and dress neatly, this includes all 
agents and subcontractors. 
•

orderly and proper manner so as not to annoy, disturb, or be offensive to others and shall not create 
a disturbance or distraction during any event at the Fairgrounds. 
• 
Contractor’s employees must always conduct themselves in a courteous manner to the public and 
Fairgrounds staff. 
• 
Contractor’s employees must enter and exit the facility at the approved and pre-determined 
entrances and exits as noted by Fairgrounds.

requirements, whether local, state, or federal. 
• 
Contractor will obtain and hold all pertinent permits and licenses. It is the responsibility of the 
Contractor to determine the applicability of any rule, regulation or other requirement. Fees are the 
responsibility of the Contractor. 
• 
Contractor must follow schedules and instructions provided by Fairgrounds during the life of the 
contract. 
• 
Contractor will provide necessary equipment and service within the specified times for any work.
