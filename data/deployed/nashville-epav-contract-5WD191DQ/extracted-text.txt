THIS AGREEMENT, made and entered into on this, the go day
of October , 1989, by and between the Metropolitan Nashville
Airport Authority, a public corporation in the State of Tennessee
(hereinafter referred to as "Authority"), and SOUTHWEST AIRLINES fo.
(hereinafter referred to as "Airline."

WITNESS ETH:

WHEREAS, the Metropolitan Nashville Airport Authority is the
owner and operator of the Nashville Metropolitan Airport
(hereinafter referred to as "“Airport"), and certain air
navigational facilities incidental thereto, situated in Davidson
County, State of Tennessee; and .

WHEREAS, SOUTHWEST AIRLINES is engaged in the business of
transporting persons, property and cargo to and from the Airport
by air on a scheduled basis:

WHEREAS, at the request of the _dirlines serving the
Nashville International Airport, the Authority has constructed
ground service equipment (GSE) facilities to support airline
operations; and,

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants,
terms, conditions, privileges, obligations and agreements herein
contained, the Authority and the Airline hereby mutually
undertake, promise and agree, each for itself, and its successors
and assigns, as follows: :

1 ,


SECTION 1. OPERATING RIGHTS - Airline is permitted to use,
in common with others authorized so to do, the Ground Service
Equipment Fueling Facilities of Nashville International Airport
for its ground service equipment required in connection with
support activities for a scheduled Airline business.

SECTION 2. OPERATING FACILITIES - For the fueling of its

, ground service equipment, Airline shall use the areas on the
Airport suitable for such activities as shall be designated by
the Authority. The use of such facilities shall be subject to
such specific rules and modifications as the Authority may
require as the exigencies of the immediate situation may dictate
for the safe and proper handling of vehicular traffic on the
apron area.

SECTION 3. TERM - The term of this Agreement shall be for a
period of one (1) year, commencing on the effective date of this
Agreement and shall be automatically renewed on a year-to-year
basis. Provided however, this Agreement may be terminated by
either party by giving written notice to the other party at least
thirty (30) days prior to any annual renewal date hereof.

SECTION 4. OPERATING RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE AUTHORITY — The
Authority will operate, manage. and administer the GSE Facilities
in support of Airline operations at the Airport, and shall be
responsible for the following:

1. Fuel tank installation, inspection, testing and

registration of fuel tanks and equipment.

2. Ordering and maintaining all fuel and glycol levels for

the GSE Facility, including keeping the product free .
from contaminants after it is placed into and while
being stored in the tanks owned and operated by the

: Authority. Fuel/Glycol will be billed directly to the

Airline by the supplier/distributor. The Authority
will provide the supplier/distributor the percentage of

percentage the Airline hereby agrees and admits is
accurate. The percentage will be based upon the
carriers usage during the previous reporting period.
3. Maintaining records of all fuel dispensed and inventory
levels. System access cards will be individually coded
and issued to each user. :
: 4. Billing each Airline monthly a per gallon fee for debt
service and operation and maintenance costs identified
‘with the operation of the GSE Facility.
5. Maintaining the facilities in a clean, neat and
sanitary condition. .
6. Repairing and maintaining the equipment and fixtures
associated with the operation of the GSE Facility.
7. Providing for sanitary trash removal.
8. Providing sweeping, snow removal and roadway on the
surrounding facility.
SECTION 5. PRIVILEGES AND OBLIGATIONS OF AIRLINE - As an
Airline Tenant at the Nashville International Airport and a user
of the GSE Facility, Airline will be responsible for the
following:
1. Testing the product upon delivery, before being pumped
into the Authority tanks for purity and = non-
contamination. The individual or carrier who will be
responsible for testing the product will be decided by
the station managers of all the carriers using the GSE
Facility. ; ,
2. Insuring that Authority tanks are not contaminated
during product delivery, testing, or dispensing. .
3. Pumping the fuel/glycol from the Authority owned and
operated tanks into Airline approved trucks and .
. equipment. ‘
4. Mixing the glycol. properly according to Airline
specifications.

5. Maintaining the purity and non-contamination of the
product once the fuel/glycol has been removed from the
Authority tanks.

6. Fueling and or de-icing the aircraft properly.

7. Fuel/Glycol spillage or contamination of airport-
property caused as a result of user handling of
fuel/glycol.

SECTION 6. USER FEES - Airline shall pay to the Authority as
compensation for construction and use of the GSE Facility the
following: .

1. A per gallon fee established by the Authority to cover

the construction cost, debt service and Operation and
Maintenance Costs identified with the operation of the
GSE Facility, as agreed in the approved MII Ballot
titled "Construct Automotive Fueland Glycol Storage
Facilities."

These user fees may be adjusted from time to time to
reflect, as closely as possible, the actual debt service and
Operation and Maintenance Costs identified with the operation of
the GSE Facility.

SECTION 7. INDEMNIFICATIONS AND INSURANCE - The Airline,
under the terms of this Agreement, will not be in control or
possession of the Airport, and Airline does not assume
responsibility for the control or operation of the Airport or for
the physical or other conditions of same. It is expressly
understood and agreed by and between the Authority and Airline
that the Airline is, and shall be, an independent contractor and ,
operator responsible to all parties for all of its act or ;
omissions and Authority shall not in any way be responsible
therefor.

Airline agrees to defend and to hold and save Authority

” sufficient primary liability insurance with a reputable insurance
company licensed to conduct business in the state of Tennessee.

SECTION 8. RULES, REGULATIONS AND CONDITIONS - The exercise
of the use of the GSE Facility is conditioned upon strict
compliance at all times during the term hereof with the
following:

, (a) All Federal rules and regulations pertaining to
Airline’s operations at Nashville International
Airport. :

: (b) All applicable laws of the State of Tennessee,
Ordinances of The Metropolitan Government of Nashville
and Davidson County, and the rules and regulations made
by the Authority.

SECTION 9. ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION - Airline agrees to
operate under this Agreement So as to fully protect the Authority
and its environment from any environmental pollution. Airline
specifically agrees to comply with all laws, and to obey all
rules, regulations, or administrative orders of agencies of The
Metropolitan Government of Nashville and Davidson County, The
State of Tennessee and The United States as these laws, rules,
regulations and administrative'orders may now exist and as they
may be hereafter adopted. Airline further agrees to abide by all
rules and regulations adopted by Authority relating to protection
of the environment. :

SECTION 10. ATTORNEYS’ FEES - Should either party bring suit
to enforce any of the terms ‘of the Agreement, the prevailing
party shall be entitled to recover court costs and reasonable
attorneys’ fees. ,

SECTION 11. MODIFICATION - This Agreement may not be
modified unless such modification is in writing and signed by

_, both parties to the Agreenent. |

by a construed in accordance with the laws of the State of
