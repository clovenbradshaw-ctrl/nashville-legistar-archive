CONSTRUCTION MANAGER AGREEMENT 
This agreement is between the Metropolitan Government of Nashville and Davidson 
County (Metro) and Bell & Associates Construction, LLC (CM) located at 1000 Health Park 
Drive, Suite 150, Brentwood, TN, 37027. The contract term will begin on the date this contract is 
approved by all required parties and filed in the Metropolitan Clerk’s Office.  The contract term 
will end upon project completion.  In no event shall the term of this contract exceed sixty (60) 
months from the date of filing with the Metropolitan Clerk’s Office.   
1. General References.
1.1. “Owner” and “Metro” shall mean the Metropolitan Government of Nashville and
Davidson County, Tennessee.
1.2. “CM” and “Construction Manager” shall mean Bell & Associates Construction, LLC.
1.3. The “Project” shall mean Phase II of Fair Park at The Fairgrounds Nashville.
1.4. The “Architect” shall mean Adkisson & Associates Architects, Inc..
1.5. The “Contract Documents” mean this Agreement, any general or supplemental
conditions, Drawings, Specifications, addenda issued prior to execution of this 
Agreement, and any modifications, including, upon its execution, those documents 
comprising the change order establishing the GMP.   
1.6. “Substantial Completion” means the point at which the Project is at a level of 
completion in strict compliance with this Agreement such that Metro or its designee can 
enjoy beneficial use or occupancy and can use or operate it in all respects for its intended 
purpose. Partial use or occupancy of the Project shall not result in the Project being 
deemed Substantially Complete and such partial use or occupancy shall not be evidence 
of Substantial Completion.  
1.7. "Final Completion" means that point at which the Project is 100% complete and in 
conformance with the Agreement.

provided herein.  Costs that would cause the GMP to be exceeded shall be paid by

4. Scheduling. 
4.1. 
General. 
To ensure adequate planning and execution of the Work and to assist Metro in appraising 
and evaluating the progress of the Work, within ten (10) calendar days after Notice to 
Proceed, CM shall prepare and submit for Metro’s approval, a Construction Schedule for 
the Work, which shall provide for expeditious and practicable execution of the Work and 
be based on the Critical Path Method (“CPM”).  The Schedule shall be a detailed graphic 
representation of all significant aspects of the Construction Plan.  The level of detail shall 
be such that no activity shall be longer than 14 calendar days, with the exception of 
procurement activities as indicated in subsequent paragraphs. 
 
The Schedule shall be a computer-produced report utilizing commercially available 
scheduling software approved by Metro.  The Schedule data shall be categorized in such 
way as to indicate components of work as directed by Metro.   
 
The Schedule shall indicate a late completion date for the Project that is no later than the 
Project's required completion date.  All activity dates shall be given in calendar days.  For 
all major equipment and materials fabricated or supplied for this project, the Schedule 
shall show a sequence of activities including: 
 
Preparation of Shop Drawings and Sample submissions; 
Review of Shop Drawings and Samples; 
Shop fabrication and delivery; 
Erection and/or installation; and 
Project closeout. 
 
Within five calendar days of submission of the Schedule, CM and its major 
Subcontractors shall participate in a conference with Metro to review and evaluate the 
Schedule.  Any revisions necessary as a result of this review shall be resubmitted for 
review within seven calendar days after the conference.  After approval, CM shall provide 
the following to Metro: 
 
Three copies of the completed Detailed Construction Progress Schedule in 
a graphical CPM format; 
Three copies of the completed Detailed Construction Progress Schedule 
tabular reports indicating Activity Number, Activity Description, Activity 
Duration, Early Start, Early Finish, Late Start, Late Finish, and Total 
Float; and 
An electronic copy of all Schedule submissions.
