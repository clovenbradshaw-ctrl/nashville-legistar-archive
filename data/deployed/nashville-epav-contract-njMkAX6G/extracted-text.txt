COMMERCIAL GROUND TRANSPORTATION PERMIT

THIS PERMIT FOR COMMERCIAL GROUND TRANSPORTATION (the “Agreement”) is entered
into as of this 1st day of July, 2011, by and between METROPOLITAN NASHVILLE AIRPORT AUTHORITY, a
public corporation created pursuant to Tennessee Code Annotated Sections 42-4-101, et. seq., (the “Authority”), and

3J Limo , a company licensed to do business in the State of Tennessee (the “Operator”).

WHEREAS, Authority is the owner and operator of the Nashville International Airport (the “Airport”) in
Nashville, Tennessee; and

WHEREAS, Operator desires to operate a commercial ground transportation business at the Airport; and

WHEREAS, Authority has agreed to allow Operator to conduct its business at the Airport subject to the terms
of this Agreement;

NOW, THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the terms and conditions of this Agreement, and other good
and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, Authority and Operator
hereby agree as follows:

1. Policy. This Agreement is subject in all respects to the terms and conditions of the Commercial
Ground Transportation Policy of the Authority in effect on the date of this Agreement and as amended and modified
from time to time. For purposes of this Agreement, the term “Policy” shall mean such Commercial Ground
Transportation Policy of Authority and all amendments and modifications thereto from time to time. Operator hereby
acknowledges receipt of a copy of the Policy as in effect on the date of this Agreement. Capitalized terms not otherwise
defined in this Agreement shall have the meanings ascribed to them in the Policy.

2. Permit. Subject to the terms of the Policy and this Agreement, Authority hereby grants a revocable,
non-exclusive Permit to Operator for the following commercial ground transportation operator classification:

CLASS II - OFF-AIRPORT LIMOUSINE OPERATOR

(a) During the term of this Agreement, Operator shall have a non-exclusive, revocable license solely to (i)
operate Limousines that transport patrons to and from the Airport on a prearranged basis for the convenience of
passengers utilizing the Airport and patrons and tenants of the Airport; (ii) to use common-use Airport roadways for
ingress and egress, subject to the Policy and other Applicable Laws; (iii) to park Operator’s Limousines in the non-
exclusive Commercial Loading Zones designated by Authority on the ground transportation level of Terminal for twenty
(20) minutes per trip to the Airport for the purpose of loading patrons; and (iv) to unload patrons in the non-exclusive
Commercial Loading Zones designated by Authority on the passenger departing level and ground transportation level of
the Terminal.

(b) Operator’s Commercial Vehicles waiting to load or unload passengers must be positioned in the
Commercial Loading Zones designated by Authority for Commercial Vehicles of that type, and shall remain therein only
for the period herein provided. Operator shall not leave any Commercial Vehicle unattended while parked in any
Commercial Loading Zone at the Airport. Operator shall not load and/or unload on the passenger arriving level of
the Terminal except by the prior written consent of Authority.

(c) Authority shall issue to Operator one Decal per Commercial Vehicle of Operator, as specified in
Operator’s approved application for this Agreement. The Decal shall be a visual indicator and control device installed
by Authority designating that such Commercial Vehicle is authorized by Authority to operate at the Airport. A Decal
must be permanently affixed to the right rear bumper of each Commercial Vehicle of Operator that operates at the
Airport. Each Decal shall be and remain the property of Authority, and removal of a Decal from the Commercial
Vehicle to which it was originally affixed or the transfer of a Decal to any other vehicle shall result in the immediate
termination of this Agreement without further notice, opportunity to cure or other process whatsoever. In the event
that Operator discontinues using a Commercial Vehicle bearing a Decal belonging to the Airport, Operator shall
present such Vehicle to Authority so that Authority may remove the Decal from such Commercial Vehicle. Until a
Decal is removed from a Commercial Vehicle of Operator, Operator shall continue to be responsible for fees payable
with respect to such Commercial Vehicle under the terms and conditions of this Agreement.

(d) Additionally, Authority shall issue to Operator one AVI Transponder Tag per Commercial Vehicle


of Operator, as specified in Operator’s approved application for this Agreement. The AVI Transponder Tag shall be a
control device installed by Authority on each Commercial Vehicle of Operator for the purpose of recording Trip
activity of each Commercial Vehicle operated at the Airport. Each AVI Transponder Tag shall be and remain the
property of Authority, and removal of an AVI Transponder Tag from the Commercial Vehicle to which it was
originally affixed or the transfer of an AVI Transponder Tag to any other vehicle shall result in the immediate
termination of this Agreement without further notice, opportunity to cure or other process whatsoever. In the event
that Operator discontinues using a Commercial Vehicle bearing an AVI Transponder Tag belonging to the Airport,
Operator shall present such Vehicle to Authority so that Authority may remove the AVI Transponder Tag from such
Vehicle. Until an AVI Transponder Tag is removed from a Commercial Vehicle of Operator, Operator shall continue
to be responsible for fees payable with respect to such Commercial Vehicle under the terms and conditions of this
Agreement.

(e) Operator shall immediately report to Authority all changes, additions or deletions in its fleet of
Commercial Vehicles, including vehicle length, width, seats, color, make, model, year, license tag number and
vehicle identification number (VIN).
