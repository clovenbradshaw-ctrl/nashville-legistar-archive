MONTH-TO-MONTH EXTENSION AGREEMENT
TO CONCESSION AGREEMENT FOR PUBLIC PAY TELEPHONE
AND
PREPAID CALLING CARD VENDING SERVICE

THIS MONTH-TO-MONTH EXTENSION AGREEMENT TO CONCESSION AGREEMENT FOR
PUBLIC PAY TELEPHONE AND PREPAID CALLING CARD SERVICE, dated aYe hig { ,
200_'7 (“Extension”), extends that certain Concession Agreement for Public Pay Telephone and
Prepaid Calling card Service (the “Agreement”), dated May 23, 2002, by and between
Metropolitan Nashville Airport Authority, a public corporation existing under the laws of the State
of Tennessee (the “Authority”), and Global Tel*Link Corporation, a Delaware corporation having
its principal place of business at 2609 Cameron Street, Mobile, AL 36607 (“Concessionaire”),
AT&T Corp.'s assignee and successor-in-interest to the Agreement.

In consideration of the mutual covenants and promises described below, and other good
and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged,
Authority and Concessionaire agree as follows:

1) The Agreement will be extended and administered on a month-to-month basis pending the

Authority’s publishing of a Request For Proposals (RFP) for the Public Pay Telephone and

Prepaid Calling Card vending service. Concessionaire will continue to operate public pay

. telephone service and prepaid calling card vending service within the Nashville

International Airport (“Airport”) terminal during this extension.

2) Concessionaire will pay the Authority on a commission-only basis, pursuant to Section

17.2 of the Agreement, as specified:

(a) Thirty percent (30%) commission on public payphone revenue;
(b) Thirty-five percent (35%) commission on prepaid calling card revenue.
3) Pursuant to Section 17.2 of the Agreement, the Minimum Annual Guarantee payment is
. eliminated. .
4) There are no rental or other fees associated with the Assigned Area.

Capitalized terms not defined in the Extension have,the same meaning as in the
Agreement. Except as set forth above, there is no other revision or amendment to the
Agreement or the obligations of Authority and Concessionaire, and the Agreement remains in full
force and effect. *
