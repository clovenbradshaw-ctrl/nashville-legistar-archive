 
MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING 
 
Contract No. 26-52-367 
THIS MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING (“Memorandum”) is made and 
entered into by and between THE METROPOLITAN GOVERNMENT OF NASHVILLE AND 
DAVIDSON COUNTY, ACTING BY AND THROUGH THE ELECTRIC POWER BOARD OF 
SAID GOVERNMENT and operating under the service name of Nashville Electric Service 
(“NES”), and EAST BANK DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY (“EBDA”), an agency of the 
Metropolitan Government of Nashville and Davidson County.  
WHEREAS, NES and EBDA endeavor to partner to place any mutually agreed upon 
portion of the local area distribution system in the Central Waterfront District, detailed in Exhibits 
A and B hereto (the “Project Area”), in an underground duct bank. 
NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the premises, the mutual promises, and 
obligations of the parties, the parties have agreed and do hereby agree as follows: 
1. 
NES agrees to assist EBDA in placing any mutually agreed upon portion of the 
local area distribution line in an underground duct bank (the “Work”) in the Project Area. 
2. 
EBDA agrees to coordinate with NES in the design process, carry financial 
responsibility, and provide the necessary engineering design and construction drawings and 
specifications.  EBDA agrees to provide the materials and labor to excavate the trench and 
construct the NES duct bank in the Project Area.  EBDA will also provide as-built topographic 
ground survey information to aid in identifying the final route alignment. 
3. 
NES agrees to provide necessary and timely design review and draft construction 
specifications.  NES agrees to carry financial responsibility and provide materials and labor for the 
installation, testing, activation, and maintenance of underground electrical distribution equipment.  
All Work performed by EBDA and their contractors must meet standard NES requirements, 
including a final inspection, before being accepted by NES.  The parties agree that the details and 
conditions in Exhibits A and B are subject to change as the project progresses.  As such, each party 
will be responsible only for its own costs associated with any additional changes or variations from 
Exhibits A and B. 
4. 
EBDA agrees to keep NES up to date on the aforementioned Work by means of bi-
weekly progress reports and/or inclusion on project progress meetings.   
5. 
If any electrical service requires relocation due to moving electrical distribution 
lines from overhead to underground, then EBDA agrees to coordinate and be responsible for any 
costs required to relocate the customer’s service.  NES agrees to assist in the re-design process of 
any relocated services. 
6. 
This Memorandum embodies the entire agreement between the parties hereto and 
supersedes any prior understandings or written or oral agreements between the parties concerning
