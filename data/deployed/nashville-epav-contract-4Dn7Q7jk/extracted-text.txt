THIS FIRST AMENDMENT TO LEASE AGREEMENT, is made, entered into,
and effective this first day of September 2011, by and between the METROPOLITAN
NASHVILLE AIRPORT AUTHORITY, a public corporation existing under the laws of the
State of Tennessee, hereinafter referred to as "Authority", and BELLSOUTH
TELECOMMUNICATIONS, LLC hereininafter referred to as “Lessee”.
WITNESSETH:
| WHEREAS, Authority and Lessee entered into a Lease Agreement dated May 28, 1987,
| ("the Agreement"), granting Lessee the right to operate a telephone company’s central office and
or/wire center at the Passenger Terminal Building (“Terminal”); and
WHEREAS, the Agreement has been amended on six (6) subsequent occasions to reflect inter |
alia extensions to the term and/or other operational issues; and |
WHEREAS, the parties desire to further amend the Agreement to provide for (i) the
extension of the agreement for five (5) additional years and two (2) five (5) year renewal options.
NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the premises, mutual covenants, terms and
conditions contained herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and
sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Agreement is hereby amended in the following
respects only:
1. ARTICLE II. TERM Sections 2.1 and 2.2 are hereby deleted in their
entirety and substituting in lieu thereof the following:
The term of this Agreement shall commence as of the Effective Date and continue
through August 31, 2016 (the “Term”). Lessee is hereby granted two (2) five (5)
year renewal options unless either party gives the other party notice of its intent
not to renew at least twenty-four months (24) prior to the expiration of the then
current Term.
2. ARTICLE VII. RENTALS, FEES & CHARGES Section 7.1 is hereby
modified by deleting the figure “Twenty Three Thousand Seven Hundred Sixty
Eight Dollars ($23,768.00): and substituting in lieu thereof the figure “Thirty Seven
Thousand Six Hundred Fifty and 84/100 Dollars ($37,650.84).”
3. ARTICLE VII. RENTALS, FEES & CHARGES Section 7.6 is hereby
modified by adding the following:
“All charges under this Lease shall be billed by Landlord within one (1) year from
the end of the calendar year in which the charges were incurred; any charges
beyond such period shall not be billed by Landlord, and shall not be payable by


Tenant, unless otherwise agreed to by both parties. The provisions of this
subparagraph shall survive the termination or expiration of this Lease.”
This First Amendment shall be effective September 1, 2011, and shall continue for the full
term of the Agreement unless otherwise modified or amended in writing.
In the event of any conflict, inconsistency or incongruity between the provisions of this First
Amendment and any provisions of the Agreement, the more restrictive on Lessee shall in all
respects govern and control.
It is expressly agreed by the parties that this First Amendment modifies the Agreement, but
unless specifically modified herein, all other terms, conditions and provisions thereof shall remain
in full force and effect.
