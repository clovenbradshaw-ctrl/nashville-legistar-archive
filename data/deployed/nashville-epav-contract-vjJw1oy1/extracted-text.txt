2023 Nashville Farmers’ Market  
Market House Vendor Rules and Policy Handbook 
 
Farmers, makers, and small businesses are the cornerstone of the Nashville Farmers' Market (“NFM” or 
“Farmers Market”). Since its inception in the 1800s, NFM has offered these small businesses 
(“vendors”) the opportunity to vend 362 days a year. In the summer, NFM also operates an offsite 
market at Vanderbilt Medical Center. 
 
Nashville Farmers' Market vendor benefits: 
 
• 
A welcoming, diverse community of vendors and supporters 
• 
More than 1,500 surface parking spaces conveniently located within half a mile 
• 
Opportunity to be featured in NFM seasonal events, workshops, advertising, etc. 
• 
Marketing support 
 
About the Nashville Farmers' Market: NFM operates daily as a retail hub and small business 
incubation center for farmers, artisans, and small businesses that desire to serve the region. NFM is 
located on a 12-acre property in the urban core of Nashville, within walking distance of many local 
neighborhoods, including Germantown, Salemtown, the Gulch and the downtown district. The public 
facility includes two outdoor sheds, a garden center, and a food hall offering an international foods 
market, local artisans, and a variety of dining options. 
 
Mission Statement: Curating an inclusive destination Market Place that fosters a connection between 
our community and the farmers, foods, and artisans who contribute to our food system.

Application & Rates 
 
How to Apply 
 
• Read the NFM Market House Vendor Rules and Policy Handbook in full before applying. 
Submitting your application means you agree to comply with this Handbook and all applicable 
Nashville Farmers Market Rules. See current Nashville Farmers’ Market Board rules available at 
https://www.nashville.gov/sites/default/files/2021-03/farmers_market-board-
rules.pdf?ct=1615922761.  
• Apply at https://www.nashvillefarmersmarket.org/become-a-market-house-tenant  
• Upload the required documents for your vendor type as listed in the application. 
• Answer all questions for approval. 
• Review your application (incomplete applications will not be considered). 
 
NFM management may request additional information, a meeting, and/or product samples if considering 
you for approval.  After an internal review of applications, NFM staff will select vendors to present to 
the Nashville Farmers’ Market Board during a public meeting.  The Board makes final decisions 
regarding vendors approvals. 
 
Please Note: the application process to sell at the Nashville Farmers’ Market is competitive.  
Acceptance of vendors will be at the discretion of NFM management. Applications will be approved as 
needed by the NFM management team based on criteria including, but not limited to: 
 
• 
The needs of the Market, including product mix 
• 
Product quality and fit 
• 
Market-readiness including marketing, branding and merchandising 
• 
For existing vendors, track record with payment, attendance, marketing, conduct, past  
 
violations, etc. 
 
NFM management reserves the right to deny the application of any vendor for any reason other than 
discrimination, as outlined in the Harassment-Free Workplace policy. 
 
Required Documents 
 
Insurance: All vendors must carry general liability, including product liability, and property insurance 
at a minimum of $1 million.  Vendors shall maintain workers’ compensation insurance with statutory 
limits required by the State of Tennessee or other applicable laws and Employer’s Liability Insurance 
with limits of no less than one hundred thousand dollars ($100,000) as required by the laws of 
Tennessee, except such insurance is not required for companies with fewer than five employees.   
Vendor’s insurance shall name Metro Nashville Government and the Nashville Farmers’ Market as 
additional insured and shall maintain all other coverage as required by law.  Copies of certificates of 
insurance must be provided with the application. If insurance expires during the season, the vendor must 
provide a copy of the renewed insurance policy at the time of policy expiration. 
 
Licenses/Permits: Vendors must provide business licenses, sales tax certificates and other applicable

Grow Local Kitchen and a Commissary Kitchen in the Market House. The intended purpose of these 
facilities is to provide farmers, local businesses, and artisans with a space to produce value-added 
products for sale. The Grow Local Kitchen will be commercially approved and include retail capability 
for local start-up businesses to test their business model. Both kitchens are designed to support the 
economic development of the local food industry here at the NFM. Interested parties can rent these 
locations through the NFM. Please contact the Program Manager for information regarding these 
programs.

security concern or a lost or stolen item, please contact a member of the security team immediately.

Janitorial Services: Janitorial services are contracted to provide cleaning and sanitation of common

management or security personnel to report the issue immediately.

• Pest control

water, gas, janitorial services, and security services are an expense shared by all merchants and are 
factored into the monthly rent rate formula.

Payment 
 
Advance Payment: All merchants are expected to make payment by the 5th of each calendar month 
using check, money order, or other means approved by the NFM Finance Department.  Checks can be 
mailed to: 
 
 
NFM Finance Office 
900 Rosa Parks Blvd 
Nashville, TN 37208 
 
No cash payments will be accepted.  Checks and money orders can be mailed or dropped off in person at 
the NFM office between 9:00 am and 3:00 pm Tuesday-Friday. After hours check payments can be 
made utilizing our safe drop box outside the office door. Checks are only acceptable from merchant 
accounts in good standing. Returned (or “bounced”) checks are automatically sent to Metro Nashville 
Government’s collections department. 
It is the merchant’s responsibility to remain in good standing.  If a payment is made after the 5th of each 
month a $50 fine will be applied to your account.  Past due balances that roll into the following month 
are subject to a $250 fine. 
All other charges, including any late charges, penalties for violations, or payment for NFM property 
damage shall also be due by the 5th of the month following NFM notice of such charges.  
If you are having issues paying your rent, reach out to an NFM Staff member prior to missing a 
payment. 
 
Please contact the Finance & Operations Manager for payment information.

Market-House Day/Hours of Operation:  
 
• Building Hours: 8:00 a.m. – 8:00 p.m. 
• Merchant Required Operating Hours: 10:00 a.m. – 6:00 p.m. 
• Seven days per week excluding NFM Observed Holidays (New Year’s Day, Thanksgiving Day, 
and Christmas Day) 
• Early Market House Closure: The Market House may host events in the evenings Sundays 
through Thursdays; such special events would be held in Market House Common Areas and not 
affect business hours.  The Market House may close early for special events on weekend (Friday-
Saturday) nights if the NFM Board gives prior approval and NFM management gives tenants 30 
days’ notice.  There may be a maximum of four weekend early closures per calendar year.  In all 
of the above instances, the NFM alone shall determine whether vendors must close operations or 
may remain open for business in emergency situations.

All merchants are required to operate their businesses on all days and during all hours the NFM is open 
to the public unless otherwise communicated to and approved by the NFM management.   
 
Vendor Premises and Use: 
 
 
• Vendors’ operations must stay within boundaries of their rented, assigned space(s). The 
placement of vendor merchandise or other items in the aisles or anywhere outside the rented 
space(s) is prohibited.  
• Blocking a roadway with merchandise, equipment or for loading/unloading purposes is strictly 
prohibited.  
• In-house walkways must remain clear at all times for customers during operating hours.  
  
Events: NFM hosts several special events annually, including monthly themed Night Markets and 
weekend festivals. These events provide vendors with an opportunity to reach a large audience and 
market seasonal goods. Please refer to our website for scheduled event details. Merchants are required to 
operate their business during all events unless approved by the NFM management.    
 
Inclement Weather Policy: NFM is open rain or shine. Failure to attend on poor weather days will 
count towards your total absences. NFM management will make decisions to alter market operations and 
hours based on weather reports and on-site market conditions. It is within the authority of NFM 
management staff to close the market early, open the market late or cancel a market day due to severe or 
inclement weather.  During inclement weather events, it is up to the individual vendors to weigh factors 
and make a decision about whether or not to attend market that day. Absences due to severe or 
hazardous weather (not just rain), as determined at the sole discretion of NFM, will not count against 
market attendance records. Refunds and credits will not be provided for inclement weather closure 
except with written approval from NFM management. 
 
Sales Reporting: NFM requires sales reporting for all merchants. Monthly gross sales submissions 
should be sent to the NFM Finance Officer/Manager by the 5th day of the following calendar 
month. Repeated withholding of gross sales information constitutes a policy violation.  The Nashville 
Farmers’ Market reserves the right to conduct audits on your sales reporting without notice. 
 
Food Containers: Vendors shall only use approved NFM food containers while in operation. 
 
Alcohol: Vendors shall not serve alcohol unless vendor meets relevant licensure and additional 
insurance requirements and their license or lease reflects NFM approval to make such sales. 
 
Cleanliness:  NFM vendors are expected to uphold or exceed the USDA and Metro Health Department 
standards regarding cleanliness and safe food handling.  Failure to pass regular food safety inspection 
must be reported to NFM Management within 48 hours of said inspection. 
Signage: Vendors are required to display signage of their company name. Pricing must be listed by 
type/weight (price sheet, signs or priced on item) throughout each day. Vendors are not allowed to tape 
or wrap signs to the columns in the market house. Vendors may use TVs to display menus and other 
marketing elements. TVs can be no larger than 24” and must be muted.

Market Property and Signage: No signs, banners, nails, screws or advertising material may be placed 
on any market building, walls, or doors without written approval from NFM management.  Hand-written 
signage is not permitted in the Market House.  All signs and placement of signage must be approved by 
the NFM management.  All signage must comply with NFM’s Rules on Prohibited Items.   
 
Stanchions: If a customer line is impeding the common areas, it is the responsibility of the lessee to 
provide stanchions to provide order to the queue.  
 
 
Cameras: Although vendors are generally responsible for the security of their stations, Nashville 
Farmers’ Market has security cameras placed throughout the Market House to monitor the property as a 
whole. If a vendor or patron requests to capture video in response to a crime and the request is timely 
brought to NFM management’s attention, NFM will review relevant footage and release the footage to 
law enforcement at its discretion. Vendors may place cameras within their rented spaces only with 
written permission from NFM management, which will review camera placement and sample footage, 
among other factors. Vendors are not permitted to place cameras on Market columns or other Market 
fixtures. Vendors may only record footage within their space and may not record other spaces or 
vendors. Violation of any of these policies will result in immediate removal of cameras and can result in 
temporary suspension of business operation. 
Modifications to and Devices on Market Property:  No other physical attachments or modifications 
shall be constructed or installed on NFM property without prior approval.  No electronic device of any 
kind, including televisions, radios, security devices, satellite dishes, etc. shall be installed without prior 
NFM approval.   No loudspeakers, radios, televisions, or similar devices shall be used in a manner such 
that they can be seen or heard outside of the vendor’s Premises without NFM approval.   
 
Nuisance:  Vendors shall not make noises, create odors, or cause disturbances which could reasonably 
be found offensive by other Farmers Market licensees, employees, customers, or others, or conduct 
themselves in such a way that they disturb others’ quiet enjoyment of the Farmers Market.   
 
 
General NFM Premises Rules 
 
Parking: All vendors are required to park in designated, areas when participating at the Market.  On 
Saturdays and Sundays vendors are required to park in the nearby state parking lots to ensure adequate 
parking availability for NFM customers. (See map on next page for lot locations).   
 
Parking in fire lanes, doorways or places not designated as parking or loading zones is always 
prohibited.  Overnight parking is strictly prohibited. NFM management will provide alternative parking 
instructions in any instance when designated areas are impacted by road or lot closures. Further 
documentation—including, but not limited to, state-issued handicap placards and medical records—may 
be required upon application for such an accommodation.  Vendors who violate the parking policies of 
the market may be towed at the owner’s expense.

Loading Zone:  All loading and unloading of goods shall be done only at such times, in the areas, and 
through the entrances designated for such purposes by NFM.   Vendor property shall not be placed in the 
loading zone area for a period greater than two hours.  Vendors are to share use of the loading zone and

Garbage/Refuse:  NFM offers recycling, compost and landfill waste collection at the on-premises waste 
center. All recycling, compost and garbage must be removed from rented spaces and placed in the 
appropriate bins at the end of each day.   Prior to that point, it must be kept in appropriate containers and 
kept within each vendors’ leased premises.  Garbage must be in securely tied garbage bags.   No refuse, 
rubbish, or pallets shall be stored in the Market House common areas, including the loading zone  To 
keep the Market area clean and presentable for customers, vendors must break down boxes, store trash 
in an orderly manner, and bring any excessive refuse to the waste center as needed.  
 
Plumbing:  NFM plumbing facilities, including grease interceptors, are only to be used for purposes for 
which they were designed, and vendors shall avoid placing foreign substances inside the plumbing.  
Damage resulting from a violation of this policy shall be repaired at the cost of the vendor who, or 
whose employees, agents, or invitees, caused such damage.

physical conduct of a sexual nature when, for example:

acts; denigrating jokes; and written or graphic material that denigrates or shows hostility or aversion 
toward an individual or group and that is placed on walls or elsewhere on the employer's premises or 
circulated in the workplace, on company time or using company equipment via e-mail, phone (including 
voice messages), text messages, tweets, blogs, social networking sites, or other means.  
 
These policies apply to all applicants’ employees and off-site markets where NFM operates, whether 
related to conduct engaged in by fellow employees or someone not directly connected to NFM (e.g., an 
outside vendor, consultant or customer). Conduct prohibited by these policies is unacceptable in the 
workplace and in any work-related setting outside the workplace, such as during business trips, business 
meetings and business-related social events. 
 
Grievance Policy: If a vendor has a grievance regarding the Market, its staff, or any member of the 
vending community, it must be submitted in a written statement to Market management within 48 hours 
of the occurrence so that the claim can be properly investigated. If the grievance is related to Market 
management in particular, please direct your written statement to the NFM Board Chair for review 
within 48 hours of the occurrence. NFM prohibits retaliation against any individual who reports 
discrimination or harassment or who participates in an investigation of such reports. The Nashville 
Farmers’ Market seeks to foster a safe and inclusive environment for all, and grievance claims will be 
pursued with the utmost concern and promptness to insure resolution.   
 
Please contact the Executive Director if you have experienced a violation of any of these rules.

Agreement & Penalties 
 
As a condition of submitting an application and vending at the NFM, vendors agree to comply with this 
Rules and Policy Handbook and with all applicable Nashville Farmers Market Board Rules, which can 
be found here: https://www.nashville.gov/sites/default/files/2021-03/farmers_market-board-
rules.pdf?ct=1615922761.   
 
Violations and Penalties: Violations or failure to comply with any NFM rules and policies will 
generally result in the following disciplinary action: 
 
• 1st Offense: A verbal warning with documentation of the policy offense. 
• 2nd Offense: A written warning and a $50 policy violation fee. 
• 3rd Offense: Additional fines, suspension, and/or termination of contract.  
 
Penalties may vary depending on the severity of the offense, and the NFM staff may apply such 
penalties at its discretion.  All violations of NFM rules, policies or directives constitute a breach of 
license to operate at the Market.  Extreme violations can result in the permanent loss of any or all NFM 
privileges and contract termination, as well as associated charges, fees, and damages. 
 
Amending Policies: NFM Policies may be altered, amended, or replaced by a majority vote of the 
Farmers’ Market Board at any regular or special meeting. Proposed changes to the Guidelines must be 
filed with the Board Chair in writing at least 7 days before the meeting during which it is to be 
considered and before the notice of such meeting has been provided to the Members. 
 
Indemnity Agreement:  In consideration of the NFM granting a license to rent, occupy, and do 
business from assigned space at the NFM, vendors agree to indemnify and hold harmless the NFM from 
and against any and all claims, demands, actions, losses, obligations, costs, charges, expenses, 
judgments, damages, and liabilities whatsoever (including, without limitation, reasonable fees and 
expenses of attorneys, expert witnesses, and other consultants), which NFM may sustain, suffer, or incur 
by reason of, in connection with, or arising out of the activities or use of Nashville Farmers’ Market 
facilities by a vendor or its employees, family, agents, contractors, or invitees.  All vendors agree hold 
the Farmers Market harmless in the event of any damage to or loss of personal property brought onto or 
located on the premises and understand that they bring such property onto the premises at their own risk.  
Vendors are responsible for their own general and product liability insurance.  
 
In the event that a vendor fails to indemnify and hold harmless as herein agreed, the Nashville Farmers’ 
Market shall have full rights to defend, pay or settle said claim on its behalf without notice to the vendor 
and with full rights to recourse against such vendor for all fees, costs, expenses, and payments made or 
agreed to be paid to discharge said claim. In the event of default, vendors agree to pay all reasonable 
legal fees and expenses necessary to enforce their licenses to do business at the Market House.
