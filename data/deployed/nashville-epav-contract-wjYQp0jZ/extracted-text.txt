MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING BETWEEN 
 
Metropolitan Nashville-Davidson County Office of Emergency Management 
 
AND 
 
The Metropolitan Nashville Airport Authority  
 
FOR 
 
Integrated Public Alert & Warning System (IPAWS) Coordination & Distribution 
 
 
I. 
PURPOSE 
 
This Memorandum of Understanding ("MOU") is entered into by and between the Metropolitan 
Nashville Davidson County Office of Emergency Management and the Metropolitan Nashville Airport 
Authority (collectively "the Parties") for the purpose of distribution of IPAWS messages at the request 
of the Metropolitan Nashville Airport Authority when an appropriate emergency occurs within the 
Nashville International Airport and associated properties and meets established criteria for issuing 
warnings. 
 
II. 
BACKGROUND 
 
The Integrated Public Alert & Warning System (IPAWS) is FEMA's national system for local alerting 
that provides authenticated emergency and life-saving information to the public through mobile phones 
using Wireless Emergency Alerts, to radio and television via the Emergency Alert System, and on the 
National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration's Weather Radio. 
 
III. 
UNDERSTANDING OF THE PARTIES 
 
The Metropolitan Nashville-Davidson County Office of Emergency Management will perform the 
following:  
 
1. Make a reasonable effort to distribute IPAWS messaging at the request of the 
Metropolitan Nashville Airport Authority Airport Communications Center if an 
incident meets the criteria for IPAWS distribution. 
 
2. Make a reasonable effort to distribute messages in a timely manner if the request meets 
one or all of the criteria listed in paragraph 3 below. 
 
3. Make a final decision whether a request meets one or all of the following criteria: 
 
Does the hazardous situation require the public to take immediate action? 
 
Does the hazardous situation pose a serious threat to life or property? 
 
Is there a high degree of probability the hazardous situation will occur?

4. Will maintain a designated polygon of the airport area for distribution of IPAWS 
messages. The polygon will include the major roadways outlying the airport, including

5. Provide a 24-hour phone line for accepting requests for IPAWS messaging on behalf 
of the Airport. 
 
6. Make a reasonable effort to notify the Metropolitan Nashville Airport Authority if no 
longer the Collaborative Operating Group (COG) for Davidson County. 
 
Metropolitan Nashville Airport Authority will perform the following:  
 
1. Airport Communications Center staff will strive to maintain training in IS-247.b and 
IS-251.a through the Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA) Independent 
Study Program.  
 
2. Will make reasonable effort to ensure request will meet one or all of the following 
criteria: 
 
Does the hazardous situation require the public to take immediate action? 
 
Does the hazardous situation pose a serious threat to life or property? 
 
Is there a high degree of probability the hazardous situation will occur? 
3. 
MNAA will provide OEM with a list of personnel authorized to request an IPAWS 
message. Further, MNAA will provide each authorized personnel with a five-digit 
PIN to provide OEM when requesting an IPAWS message. The list detailing the 
authorized personnel with their corresponding five-digit PIN will be updated annually 
and provided to OEM.
