1. 
Purpose 
The purpose of this Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) is to formalize the terms and responsibilities contained 
in this agreement between the Metropolitan Government Department of Information Technology Services (ITS) 
and Circuit Court Clerk’s Office (CCC) as it pertains to remote and physical access to CCC managed equipment and 
data. This agreement will be used to gain a common understanding of the roles, responsibilities, and the terms of 
operation. It is intended that this will be a living document with periodic reviews and that revisions will be made 
per mutual agreement to align with changes in requirements. 
2. 
Scope 
This MOU covers the current services supplied by ITS to CCC. Any need for additional services may be covered in an 
amendment. 
3. 
Background 
ITS is charged with operating, maintaining, and monitoring Metro Government’s Primary Data Center. Equipment, 
software and data from many departments and agencies of the Metro Government are housed within the Data 
Center. Data hosted within the facility are at a range of sensitivity levels, requiring controls for CJIS (Criminal Justice 
Information Systems), HIPAA (Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act), and other regulatory and 
statutory obligations.  Thus, to maintain the security, availability, and reliability of services for all these departments 
and agencies, an agreement is required between those agencies requiring physical access to the data center. 
4. 
Assumptions 
ITS and CCC will collaboratively: 
1. Have appropriate processes in place to support the services identified in this MOU. 
2. Abide by process and standards established for the services described in this MOU. 
3. Support open communications and access related to operational processes related to the identified 
services. 
4. Provide each other timely notification of any changes in level of service, change in process, security 
breaches, or change in communication processes. 
5. Keep current the responsibilities identified for each agency. 
6. Adhere to all pertinent information security policies or regulation requirements. 
7. Review the MOU, services and all associated process, procedure, and standards annually.

5. 
Service Details 
1. Hosting. ITS will house CCC servers and associated devices such as tape libraries and storage area networks 
in Metro's Primary Data Center. ITS will designate a suitable area within the Primary Data Center for CCC 
equipment. Racks storing CCC managed equipment will only be used to house CCC managed equipment.  
2. Co-housing with JIS. Equipment may be housed in Justice Information Systems’ (JIS) racks with the 
permission of JIS. That permission will be worked out between JIS and CCC and access to those cabinets 
provided through JIS  
3. Monitoring. CCC is responsible for monitoring CCC equipment that is housed within the Metro primary 
Data Center. ITS strongly recommends that CCC staff utilize remote monitoring tools to ensure the 
availability of services hosted on CCC equipment. 
4. Site Security. ITS, through the Davidson County Sheriff’s Office will provide for 24x7 physical security of 
the primary data center. CCC will follow all access and security policies when accessing ITS facilities. 
5. Power. ITS is responsible for providing and fully supporting filtered, redundant power to supply CCC needs 
within the data center as a part of this agreement. 
6. Cooling. ITS is responsible for providing adequate conditioned HVAC for CCC equipment. 
7. Connectivity. ITS will provide connectivity of equipment to the CCC equipment and sites. 
8. Rack Planning. CCC is responsible for providing a drawing or schematic of proposed configurations for each 
rack to be submitted for review and approval by the ITS Data Center Manager. CCC is also responsible for 
modifying the drawings to reflect any changes and for gaining approval of any physical modifications prior 
to submission of change control forms. CCC will notify ITS within 30 days of any changes made. 
9. Rack Configurations. CCC will coordinate efforts with Metro ITS to ensure proper power redundancy and 
cooling in CCC managed equipment racks. 
10. Inventory. CCC is responsible for maintaining accurate inventory records of all equipment housed in the 
data center. 
11. ITS will maintain schematics that include CCC's equipment, security camera locations, and secured physical 
entry locations of the Data Center. Any changes to the location of this equipment must be provided to 
Metro Nashville Police IT Director prior to the actual change. 
12. Cabling. All cabling except patch cabling is done by ITS. ITS will provide patch cables. All cabling will be 
neat using proper cable management techniques and will be monitored by the ITS Data Center Manager. 
13. Change Control. CCC will be an active participant in the Metro ITS’ Change Advisory Board (CAB) and 
participate in ITS’ change control process on any changes that affect the Metro ITS data center 
infrastructure. 
14. Production. CCC is responsible for all aspects of production processing associated with CCC equipment. 
15. Physical Access. CCC authorized personnel shall have access into the Metro Primary Data Center to 
conduct business as needed. Access to the Primary Data Center will only be granted to CCC personnel who 
have been approved by the CCC Director. CCC authorized personnel will follow the same access 
requirements and protocols to enter the Primary Data Center, as those followed by authorized ITS 
personnel. Anyone not approved by the CCC Director, such as other staff or vendors who need access to 
the Primary Data Center to work on CCC equipment, must be escorted by authorized CCC personnel at all 
times, must follow sign-in procedures, and must have passed a background check. 
16. ITS personnel will not access CCC equipment stored in the Primary Data Center, unless instructed to do so 
by the CCC IT Manager, or in the event of a critical emergency. At a critical emergency, the CCC IT Director 
will be notified of the situation within 30 minutes of access by ITS personnel.

18. Data Center Access Logs. Per CJIS requirements, ITS will maintain a log of all persons entering the Primary 
Data Center. This list will include the scanned in staff as well as individuals signing in by electronic devices 
or hard copy logs. This list will be furnished to CCC on a monthly basis or upon request by CCC and sent to 
the Director of Metro Nashville Police IT. 
19. Background Check Requirement. All personnel who have potential access to CCC equipment or data, 
either physically or remotely, must successfully pass MNPD's initial background check and recurring 
background checks as provided by Metro Nashville Police IT. 
20. Data Center Use Policy and Standards. ITS is responsible for developing, maintaining, and providing CCC 
with Data Center Use requirements and will notify CCC prior to any changes to these requirements. CCC is 
responsible for following the ITS Data Center Policy (see Attachment A) with the following exceptions:  
a. 3.4: Data Center will be available 24/7 for unannounced inspections by CCC or FBI/TBI/MNPD to 
comply with CJIS Security Policy and ITS Service Level Agreement for CCC. 
b. 4.9: No security systems, infrastructure monitoring, and/or safety systems within the Data Center 
shall be altered in any way unless it is specifically the responsibility of the Authorized Data Center 
Personnel to do so; however, CCC shall retain the right to alter the systems that it owns without 
the need to collaborate with the Authorized Data Center Personnel 
c. 
5.1: CCC staff will consult with Metro ITS on equipment addition/removal prior to work being 
started, with the request that those changes go through Metro Government’s Change process.
