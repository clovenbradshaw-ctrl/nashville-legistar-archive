AMENDMENT 1 TO THE AMENDED AND RESTATED DES MANAGEMENT
AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE
METROPOLITAN GOVERNMENT OF NASHVILLE AND DAVIDSON COUNTY
AND CONSTELLATION NEW ENERGY, INC.
WHEREAS, the Metropolitan Government of Nashville and Davidson County ("Metro") and
Constellation New Energy, Inc. ("Constellation") are parties to an agreement (the "Agreement")
dated on or about December 6, 2005, under which Constellation operates and maintains the
Nashville District Energy System (the "System); and
WHEREAS, 
the parties propose to amend the Agreement as set forth herein.
NOW, 
THEREFORE, THE PARTIES AGREE AS FOLLOWS:
1. 
All of the terms of the Agreement not specifically modified herein shall remain in
full force and effect.
2. 
The term of the Agreement is hereby renewed and extended (the "First Renewal
Term") through December 17, 2020. This Amendment does not preclude the
possibility of further renewals and extensions as provided by the Agreement.
3. 
During the First Renewal Term, the Metropolitan Government shall have the right
to terminate the Agreement upon 90 days' written notice to Constellation.
4. 
The term of the Amended and Restated Fuel Purchase Contract between the
parties, which by its terms is coterminous with the Agreement, is likewise hereby
renewed and extended through December 17, 2020 and likewise may be
terminated upon 90 days' written notice from Metro to Constellation.

savings.
