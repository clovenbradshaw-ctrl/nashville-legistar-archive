PERMISSIVE USE AND RIGHT OF ENTRY AGREEMENT
I
I
THIS AGREEMENT is made and entered |into by and between THE METROPOLITAN
NASHVILLE AIRPORT AUTHORITY, hereinafter "GRANTOR," THE METROPOLITAN
GOVERNMENT OF NASHVILLE AND DAVIDSON COUNTY and the METROPOLITAN
TRANSIT AUTHORITY, 
hereinafter "GRANTEES".
WHEREAS, GRANTOR is the owner of the following real property:
Metropolitan Nashville Airport Authority property in Lot 3, Map 107, Parcel 050, as
recorded in Instrument Number PL-20140624 0054937 at the Metropolitan Planning
Commission of Nashville and Davidson County, and
Metropolitan Nashville Airport Authority property in Lot 3, Map 107, Parcel 050, as
recorded in Instrument Number PL-20140624 0054937 at the Metropolitan Planning
Commission of Nashville and Davidson County, Tennessee, and
Metropolitan Nashville Airport Authority property in Lot 4, Map 108, Parcel 332, as
recorded in Instrument Number PL-20140624 0054937 at the Metropolitan Planning
Commission of Nashville and Davidson County, Tennessee,
which consists of property adjacent to the right of way of Murfreesboro Road, and which is more
particularly described in Exhibits A1-A3, attached hereto; and
WHEREAS, the GRANTEES are pursuing a public project along Murfreesboro Road, which
includes the construction of bus stops, sidewalk improvements, traffic signalization
infrastructure, including fiber optic conduit, and related drainage improvements; and,
WHEREAS, the GRANTEES and/or its agents, representatives or contractors wish to enter said
property for the purpose of said public project; and
I
WHEREAS, such entry shall be confined to the areas as are approximately shown on the
drawings attached as Exhibits B1-B3, hereto (the "Permissive Use Area"); and
WHEREAS, 
it is anticipated by the parties that at or prior to the conclusion of the public project,
GRANTOR will grant to the GRANTORS an easement with respect to the Permissive Use Area'
or portions thereof.
NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of One Dollar ($1.00) cash in hand paid each to the other,
and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which is hereby
acknowledged, the parties agree and contract as follows:
1. For the term of this agreement, GRANTOR grants to GRANTEES and their agents,
representatives and contractors permission to use and enter upon GRANTOR'S
property only for the purposes outlined above.

2. GRANTEES agree that any exca;Vation on the site near utility lines shall be
coordinated with the appropriate Me.tro agency, and/or its agents.
3. In consideration for the use of the Premises, GRANTOR does not waive any claim
for damage of any kind resulting from the negligence, recklessness, or any
wrongdoing of any agent, representative or contractor for GRANTEES. Provided,
however, that nothing in this agreement is intended to waive any GRANTEES'
immunity under the Tennessee Governmental Tort Liability Act or under any other
applicable law and any claim for damage shall not apply in an amount in excess of the
minimum limits of insurance for governmental entities set forth in Term. Code Ann. §
29-20-403.
4. Either party may cancel this agreement upon forty-five (45) days' written notice to all
the other parties.
5. The term of this agreement shall commence from the date of execution of this
document and shall continue for an indefinite period until cancellation occurs per
paragraph 4, above, or the parties mutually agree to terminate this agreement, or upon
the grant by the GRANTOR of an easement for these purposes to the GRANTEES,
whichever first occurs.
6. Upon the completion of their use of GRANTOR'S property for the purposes outlined
above, GRANTEES shall remove any material from GRANTOR'S property placed
there as a result of the activities of GRANTEES or their agents, representatives, or
contractors, except as necessarily modified to accommodate the bus stop, sidewalk or
other infrastructure installed by GRANTEES as a part of the above-referenced public
project and shall otherwise restore GRANTOR'S property to the condition it was in
prior to the work mentioned above, except as necessarily modified to accommodate
the bus stop, sidewalk or other infrastructure installed by Grantees as a part of the
above-referenced public project.
7. Each party will be responsible for any claims arising from its negligence.
EXECUTED in duplicate, each executed instrument to be considered an original for all purposes.
