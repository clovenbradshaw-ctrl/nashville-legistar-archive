This project consists of three phases: 
• Phase I includes residency, community engagement and artwork concept 
development with youth at the existing Madison Community Center during 
June andJuly 2017. During Phase I, Artist will develop a work plan for the 
residency in collaboration with Metro Arts and Metro Parks. The residency 
with the youth will inform concept design for a site-specific public artwork for 
the new Madison Community Center. 
• Phase II includes final design development, structural design of the artwork.  
• Phase III includes fabrication and installation of the artwork.   
 
Upon contract execution, ARTIST will prepare and submit a Summer Residency 
Work Plan. Metro Arts will review and, if approved, ARTIST will conduct the 
residency work for the project. Upon completing the residency, ARTIST will 
develop a design concept (schematic design) for the public artwork. The design 
development process may include site visits and/or meeting and coordination with 
Metro Arts staff, local citizens and site design team.  
Once the ARTIST submits the proposed schematic design, METRO will review 
and if approved by the Metro Arts Commission, METRO will request structural 
(engineered) drawings of the artwork. METRO may reject the schematic 
design, approve it outright or approve it with conditions. METRO will not 
unreasonably withhold, condition or delay its approval.  METRO shall notify 
ARTIST in writing whether the design was approved or rejected or of any 
revisions to the schematic design necessary for approval within 30 business 
days of receipt of submittal. In the event that Metro Arts requests revisions to 
the schematic design for approval, ARTIST shall provide a revised schematic 
design within 30 days after METRO has notified ARTIST of the necessary 
revisions.   
If METRO approves the schematic design, ARTIST shall then create and 
provide to METRO structural drawings of the artwork as defined in section 1. D. 
Once structural drawings are approved by METRO, the project will move into 
Phase III, which includes the fabrication and installation of the artwork. METRO 
will notify ARTIST in writing to commence with fabrication of artwork.

project, not including the copyright embodied therein, referred to as “Artwork” or 
“Work” herein) in accordance with ARTIST’s Final Design Proposal (“Proposal”) 
as approved by Metro Arts. Artist shall install artwork on the Site (as the Site is 
described Exhibit E). A design revision resulting from alterations in site plan that 
were made by the design team, landscape architect or other alterations may be

Artwork Site Description 
Madison Community Center: Artwork Site 
 
 
 
The Summer Artist Residency will take place at the existing Madison Community Center at 510 
Cumberland Park under the direction of Metro Arts and Metro Parks.  
 
The final artwork will be installed in the new Madison Community Center and/or around the 30-acre 
Madison Community Park at 510 Cumberland Avenue.  The existing community center building built 
around 1965 will be replaced with a new 25,000 Square foot community center in the Madison 
Community. As one of the fastest growing neighborhoods in the Greater Nashville area, the Madison 
community takes pride in its open space and greenery and those characteristics are reflected in the plans 
for the project. The new regional center will have a gym, a dance room, a fitness center, meeting rooms 
and an indoor track. The park will have a new soccer field and playground. 
 
The Interior or Exterior artwork will be located at the new community center and should serve as a 
stimulating piece that will visually represent and contribute to the character and texture of Madison 
community while simultaneously inviting social interaction.
