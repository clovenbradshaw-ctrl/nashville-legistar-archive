KALEIDOSCOPE APPROACH TO CULTURAL (CLIMATE) ASSESSMENTS 
 
Conducting an assessment to evaluate the organization’s current climate is a critical step in identifying 
and understanding the individual and systematic issues at all levels of the organization. The goal of 
assessments is to ensure that the efforts of the leadership team, the board and all other key stakeholders have the 
desired impact on the culture and business objectives.  It is essential to establish a measure of the culture as it exists 
today, which can then be used as a baseline against which future progress can be measured. 
 
Scenario:  An area of opportunity has been identified.  Anecdotal evidence has been collected from a variety of 
sources.  Conclusions have been drawn.   
 
As leaders, when presented with this scenario, we often jump immediately into solution mode. In many cases, that 
approach serves us well.  However, when it comes to the complex, nuanced and deeply personal subjects of D&I, 
especially in a period of great change, we recommend taking a brief step back to obtain a more concrete 
current view before crafting a thoughtful strategic response.  This process garners results that are far more 
sustainable, actionable and have a more positive impact on objectives and goals. 
 
Truly understanding inherent root causes is the most crucial element of this process.  For example, is this a 
systemic issue or is it isolated to only a few key players?  Is there something else going on within the culture (systems, 
process, communication, leadership, etc.) that may be leading people to behave in counterproductive ways?  Or is it 
simply a basic lack of awareness or clarity around behavioral expectations?  The answers to these questions will drive the 
best solution.  It is our view that a tailored Cultural Assessment will work to identify the true need.   
 
Of almost equal importance to the actual outcomes and findings of the assessment is the fact and benefit that the process 
itself promotes awareness of D&I issues throughout the organization.  Asking employees to participate in surveys, 
interviews and focus groups where their opinions, perspectives and experiences are solicited tends to: 
a)  Increase their own sense of how important these issues are to the organization  
b)  Send a strong message to the employees that their opinions are valued.  
 
To determine what change levers need to be pulled in an effective strategy, we must have a clear understanding of what 
the real, root issues are for Walden University in relation to D&I.  At Kaleidoscope, we utilize three primary 
means of data collection that make up our Cultural Assessments (Climate Assessments); they are:

KALEIDOSCOPE APPROACH TO DIVERSITY EDUCATION  
 
Diversity Education is a key service we offer to clients who are launching a Diversity and Inclusion initiative which also 
connected to the organization’s mission in a meaningful way. Kaleidoscope believes that highly effective training is linked to 
organizational goals, provides skills that can be applied on the job, and is supported through systems that promote follow-
through and accountability.  We have found the most effective training is experientially based to each organization’s distinct 
culture, dynamic and workplace policies. We pride ourselves on creating a safe and open learning environment that 
encourages “real” dialogue, exchanges and questions and helps build cultural competence in employees at all levels.  Our 
training approach challenges participants in a non-attacking manner, therefore making it easy to discuss all viewpoints, both 
mainstream and less-traditional.   
 
Kaleidoscope leverages an interactive and experiential learning approach (first/third person, classroom, eLearning, 
simulation, blended etc.) where we work to identify and challenge beliefs, leave a lasting impression from the training, and 
integrate with the broader change effort in an organization where possible.  Kaleidoscope uses world-class professionals 
to design and/or facilitate our curriculum programs, and drives to create connection to your business by applying a 
pragmatic, results oriented approach. 
 
When addressing the development of a D&I curriculum, Kaleidoscope leverages our 4 I’s Education Model (see below), 
which guides the customization of a progressive, multi touch, multiyear blended learning program to build cultural 
competence at all levels.  The 4 I’s focus learning on the progressive learning stages that lead to cultural competence:  
Importance (understanding the business value), Insight (building awareness), Inclusion (skill development) and 
Integration (achieving optimal performance). These programs are highly flexible, and can include on-line content push 
pieces, pre / post learning assessments and reinforcements, and a combination of instructor led training and eLearning 
modules.
