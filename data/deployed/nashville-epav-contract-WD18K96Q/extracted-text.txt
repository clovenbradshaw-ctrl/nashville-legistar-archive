MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
This Memorandum of Understanding (the “MOU”) is entered into this G_ day of
Mar | , 2013, by and between The Metropolitan Government of Nashville and
Davidson County (“Metro”) by and through the Agricultural Extension Board and the University
of Tennessee (“UT Extension”).
WITNESSETH

WHEREAS, sections 11.801 and 11.802 of the Metropolitan Charter create the
agricultural extension board and set out the functions and duties of the Agricultural Extension
Board (“the Board”); and,

WHEREAS, among the duties of the Board are to exercise all the powers and perform all
the duties of the county agricultural extension committee provided for by Tennessee Code
Annotated, section 49-50-104, and otherwise to carry out the purpose of sections 49-50-101 to
49-50-106 of Tennessee Code Annotated by cooperating with the United States Department of
Agriculture and the appropriate agencies of the State of Tennessee; and,

WHEREAS, under the Charter, the Board is to employ and fix the compensation of such
personnel as may be necessary, within the limitations of its budget appropriation and funds
otherwise available, and perform such other duties as may be imposed upon the board by
ordinance ; and,

WHEREAS, in keeping with these charter provisions, the Agricultural Extension Board
cooperates with the University of Tennessee, which cooperates with Tennessee State University,
both agencies of the state as contemplated under state law; and,

WHEREAS, the Board employs and fixes the compensation of its personnel in
coordination with both universities and the compensation for such employees is funded both by
budget appropriations of the Metropolitan Council, as well as other sources including funding
from the universities as contemplated under the Metropolitan Charter together forming “UT

Extension”

WHEREAS, having been so funded the UT Extension staff functions as a Metro
department providing payroll and other related services to same; and

WHEREAS, it is the desire of the respective Parties that effective July 1, 2013, their
relationship be memorialized by agreement setting forth the terms and conditions under which
the Parties will operate.

NOW THEREFORE, for and in consideration of mutual promises and covenants herein
contained, the parties hereto agree as follows:
I. TERM AND COMPENSATION

1. The term of this MOU (“Term”) will commence on July 1, 2013 and continue through
June 30, 2014 and continue thereafter on an annual basis unless terminated by either
Party giving ninety (90) days written notice to the other prior to June 30 of the contract
year of that Parties intent to terminate. Hereinafter, all references to the “Term” of this
Agreement shall mean the initial term and any extensions or renewals thereof.

2. The Parties will, prior to July 1 of each year, agree to an Annual Certification of Funds
for the support of UT Extension’s work in an amount which will be Metro’s share of the
operating costs for UT Extension for the forthcoming fiscal year.

3. Any request for an increase in salaries and benefits for employees of UT Extension shall
not exceed increases in salaries and benefits which may be given to other Metro
employees for the initial term or any renewal thereof.

4. UT Extension agrees that it will, on a quarterly basis, during the Term of this MOU
furnish to Metro, a report detailing its efforts and accomplishments during the preceding
three months. The report shall be furnished on or about the first of October, January,
April and July.

5. UT Extension shall not incur costs beyond the extent of Metro’s agreed upon contribution
during the term of the MOU. Metro is not obligated to pay any additional sums incurred
in violation of this provision.

II. GENERAL CONDITIONS
The parties further agree as follows:
1. SUBCONTRACTING, ASSIGNMENT OR TRANSFER

a. Any subcontracting, assignment, delegation or transfer of all or part of the rights,
responsibilities, or interest of any party to this MOU is prohibited unless by written
consent of the other party. Neither this contract nor any of the rights and obligations
of UT Extension hereunder shall be assigned or transferred in whole or in part
without the prior written consent of Metro. The provisions of this contract shall inure
to the benefit of and shall be binding upon the respective successors and assignees of
the parties hereto. Any such assignment or transfer shall not release UT Extension
from its obligations hereunder.

b. Upon the request of any party, the subcontracting, assigning, delegating or
transferring party shall provide all documents evidencing the subcontract, assignment,
delegation or transfer.

2. ACCESS TO RECORDS
During the term of this MOU, UT Extension agrees to permit duly authorized agents and
employees of Metro to enter its offices for the purpose of inspections, reviews and audits
during normal working hours. Reviews may also be accomplished at meetings that are
arranged at mutually agreeable times and places. UT Extension will maintain all books,
documents, papers, accounting records, and other evidence pertaining to any sums paid
under this MOU and make such materials available at its offices at all reasonable times
during the Term of this MOU and for three (3) years from the date of payment under this
MOU for inspection by Metro.
3. GENERAL COMPLIANCE WITH LAWS .

This MOU will be interpreted in accordance with the laws of the State of Tennessee. By

execution of this MOU, the Parties agree that all actions, whether sounding in contract or

in tort, relating to the validity, construction, interpretation and enforcement of this MOU
will be instituted and litigated in the courts of the State of Tennessee, if brought against

Metro or in the Tennessee Claims Commission if brought against UT Extension.

4. NON-DISCRIMINATION

VII of the Civil Rights Act of 1964 and all other federal statutory laws which provide in

whole or in part that no person shall be excluded from participation or be denied benefits

of or be otherwise subjected to discrimination in the performance of this MOU or in the
| employment practices of UT Extension on the grounds of handicap and/or disability, age,
| race, color, religion, sex, national origin, or any other classification protected by federal,

5. ENTIRE AGREEMENT
This MOU represents the entire and integrated agreement between the parties and
supersedes all prior negotiations, representations or agreements, whether oral or written.
6. AMENDMENT
This MOU may be modified or amended only by written instrument signed by all parties
to this MOU.
7.  SEVERABILITY
If any provision of this MOU is held to be unlawful, invalid or unenforceable under any
present or future laws, such provision shall be fully severable; and this MOU shall then
be construed and enforced as if such unlawful, invalid or unenforceable provision had not
been a part hereof. The remaining provisions of this MOU shall remain in full force and
effect and shall not be affected by such unlawful, invalid or unenforceable provision or
by its severance here from. Furthermore, in lieu of such unlawful, invalid, or
unenforceable provision, there shall be added automatically as a part of this MOU a legal,
valid and enforceable provision as similar in terms to such unlawful, invalid or
unenforceable provision as possible.
8. NO WAIVER OF CONTRACTUAL RIGHT
No waiver of any term, condition, default, or breach of this MOU, or of any document
executed pursuant hereto, shall be effective unless in writing and executed by the party
making such waiver; and no such waiver shall operate as a waiver of either (a) such term,
condition, default, or breach on any other occasion or (b) any other term, condition,
default, or breach of this MOU or of such document. No delay or failure to enforce any
provision in this MOU or in any document executed pursuant hereto shall operate as a
waiver of such provision or any other provision herein or in any document related hereto.
The enforcement by any party of any right or remedy it may have under this MOU or
applicable law shall not be deemed an election of remedies or otherwise prevent such
party from enforcement of one or more other remedies at any time.
