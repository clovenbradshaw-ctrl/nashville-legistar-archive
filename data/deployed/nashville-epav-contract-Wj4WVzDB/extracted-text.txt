MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING BETWEEN 
THE METROPOLITAN POLICE DEPARTMENT AND 
Metro Election Commission 
     FOR EXTRA DUTY POLICE SERVICES 
 
 
This Memorandum of Understanding (“Memorandum”) is entered into on this 1st day of July 
2021 by and between the Chief of Police of the Metropolitan Police Department (hereinafter “MPD”), 
and Metro Election Commission (hereinafter “Agency”).  The authority of MPD shall be exercised 
by the Chief of Police and/or his/her designee. 
1. 
Purpose:  The purpose of this Memorandum is for MPD to provide supplemental police 
services by assigning officers, in addition to regularly assigned officers, at events or activities 
conducted within the Metropolitan Government area by a Metropolitan Government agency or 
department, at the cost of the Agency. 
2. 
Term:  This term of this Memorandum shall commence July 1, 2021 and shall end on June 
30, 2022 (inclusive).   
3. 
Event/Activity:  MPD shall assign police officer(s) and police vehicle(s), as requested, 
available, and as deemed appropriate by MPD, to provide security or traffic control services as 
requested.  In the event of disagreement on the number of officers needed or the need for 
supervisory officers, the determination of MPD’s Secondary Employment Unit personnel shall be 
final.  Services may also be provided on a periodic, as needed basis during the term of this 
Memorandum, in which case the Agency shall provide MPD with a list of events or activities and 
the number of officers and vehicles requested at least 15 days in advance. 
Designated Contact: Inquiries by Agency shall be directed to MPD’s Secondary Employment 
Unit during normal business hours (7:30 AM – 4:30 PM Monday through Friday) at (615) 880-
3278, fax number (615) 880-3059, or email at mpdseu@nashville.gov . Problems, or cancellations 
after normal business hours shall be directed to the afterhours cell phone (615) 485-6737.   
Supervision of Officers: While the Agency may generally plan the work to be done under this 
Memorandum, it shall not impose itself within the MPD’s chain of command.  Officers shall be 
under the direction of MPD and shall comply with all orders, rules and regulations of MPD and 
Metro’s Civil Service Commission while assigned to work under this Memorandum.  No provision 
of this Memorandum of Understanding shall be interpreted as conflicting with the customary 
policies and procedures of the MPD with regard to supervision, chain of command, or other 
authorities.  Officers will only engage in police related duties. 
4. 
Unsatisfactory Performance:  In the event Agency is not satisfied with the performance 
of an officer providing services pursuant to this Memorandum, Agency’s Designated Coordinator 
shall first discuss the dissatisfaction with the most senior officer present.  If Agency is still 
dissatisfied after the discussion, the Designated Coordinator shall discuss the matter with a MPD 
Secondary Employment Unit supervisor, and that office shall respond in a timely manner.  
5. 
Availability--Officers/Vehicles:   
a) This Memorandum does not guarantee the availability of officers, whose participation 
is on a volunteer basis and in accordance with MPD policies, or of vehicles or other equipment, 
but rather specifies the terms and conditions of the assignment of officers and vehicles under this 
Memorandum.  The Chief of Police and his/her designee have the discretion to determine the 
number of police officers, the type of supervision, the number of police vehicles, and any other 
equipment that will be provided.  MPD shall notify the Agency as soon as is practicable in the 
event that 1) fewer police officers and/or vehicles and/or other equipment than requested are

7. 
Compensation:  In consideration of the services provided under this Memorandum, 
Agency agrees to pay MPD the charges for providing the services. All officers working under this 
Memorandum will be voluntary participants, therefore, the time they work is over and above their 
normal police department assignments. All hours worked by officers under this Memorandum will 
be billed according to the rates specified in Attachment “A” or its current revision. Rates are 
subject to change and charges will be based upon the rates in effect at the time services are 
provided.  In the event of a rate change, a revised Attachment “A” reflecting the modified rates 
will be forwarded to the appropriate agency. A two-hour minimum will be paid to officers 
scheduled to work.  In the event a scheduled shift is canceled with less than two hours of notice, 
the two-hour minimum will apply. Use of police vehicles (marked and unmarked cars and 
motorcycles) shall be in accordance with Attachment “A”. These rates are subject to change. 
MPD personnel assigned to work pursuant to this Memorandum shall be in an on-duty status and 
shall be paid through the MPD payroll.  Agency shall promptly reimburse MPD the costs of 
providing services under this Memorandum.  
The participating officer shall be paid by MPD. MPD’s Secondary Employment Unit shall then 
forward to the Agency a request for reimbursement for said payment, including evidence of the 
payment, the amount paid, and MPD's computation of that amount. Charges will be based upon 
the rates in effect at the time services are provided.  MPD’s Secondary Employment Unit shall 
also forward to the Agency a request for reimbursement for all other charges for which 
reimbursement is due under this Memorandum.  Agency shall then promptly reimburse MPD.   
9. 
Termination:  Either party may terminate this Memorandum at any time upon thirty (30) 
days written notice to the other party.  
10. 
Compliance with laws:   The Agency agrees to comply with any applicable federal, state 
and local laws and regulations.
