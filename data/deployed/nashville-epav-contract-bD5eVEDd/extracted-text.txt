Exhibit A – Scope and Pricing 
 
Media Buys 
All media buys to promote the Nashville Fair will be negotiated with each individual media property. 
The agency fee is included in the price of each media buy. As a recognized agency, all media properties 
grant us an agency discount when paying the invoice.  
No separate fees will be charged to METRO for creating or managing the media buys. 
 
Responsibilities include: 
1. Attend meetings to assist with PR, marketing and insuring messaging alignment 
2. Meet with the various media and schedule appearances on local TV shows for Fair 
Representative 
3. Negotiate and place the media buys and provide proof of performance post Fair. 
4. Act as a point of contact with the various media outlets for remotes and news stories 
5. Provide the media buy details in writing. 
6. Traffic all creative to TV, Radio, Print and Outdoor with the correct instructions 
7. Assist in delivery of promotional items to media outlets in early September. 
8. Provide a staff member to meet media, as needed and provide copy points.  
9. Assist with duties in preparation for opening night. 
 
On-Site Activation, Moore Media will, for a fee of $2,499, handle the following: 
 
Media coverage, opening ceremonies, assistance as needed to prepare for opening the Fair  
 
Assistance as needed in production of Fair such as Ice Cream Eating Contest, Kids Events & 
Contest, etc 
 
Assistance as needed in production of Fair 
 
Assistance as needed with Theme Nights, Media personalities, various events 
 
Assist with Contest setup and with proof of performance items for sponsors, or as needed 
Graphic Design Work - as needed for projects. Price is $150/hour.  
Proof of Performance 
Media Buy Overview will be provided to Fairgrounds Representative for approval, prior to media 
placement. All media affidavits will be provided from each media property after advertising has run. All 
affidavits will be checked for accuracy, compiled, and delivered by Moore Media as proof of performance 
along with all invoice totals. 
 
 
 
 
*Any request for escalation is capped at 5% annually and documentation from the consumer price index must be submitted with 
any request as outlined in the Section 4.4 of the Master Contract.
