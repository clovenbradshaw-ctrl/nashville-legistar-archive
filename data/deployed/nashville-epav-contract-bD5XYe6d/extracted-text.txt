AMENDMENT NUMBER 1 TO CONTRACT NUMBER 429599 
BETWEEN 
THE METROPOLITAN GOVERNMENT OF NASHVILLE AND DAVIDSON COUNTY 
AND ART UP NASHVILLE 
 
 
This Amendment is entered into on the day this document is filed with the Metropolitan Clerk’s Office, 
by and between THE METROPOLITAN GOVERNMENT OF NASHVILLE AND DAVIDSON COUNTY (METRO) 
and ART UP NASHVILLE located in Nashville, TN. 
 
WITNESSETH 
WHEREAS, the parties desire to modify the terms and conditions and to add or delete certain other 
terms and conditions to their original agreement dated May 11, 2018, Metro Contract numbered 
429599, hereinafter the “CONTRACT”, the parties hereby agree as set forth below: 
 
This amendment affects the following changes to the contract: 
 
1. Amends clause 2.1 Duties and Responsibilities to read as follows: 
 
“Contractor agrees to oversee framing and installation and provide conservation services and 
technical advising for the Metro Arts Public Art Collection, a collection of original artwork by 
professional artists displayed in Metro-owned public spaces. Framing and installation services 
include Metro Arts’ Art WORKS Collection of 2-dimensional and 3-dimensional artworks and may 
also include artworks from other Metro departments after review with Metro Arts for eligibility. 
Contractor will provide conservation services and serve as a technical advisor for the Metro Arts 
Public Art Collection. Contractor’s conservation services and technical advising is available to other 
departments after review by Metro Arts for eligibility. Contractor will be knowledgeable of art 
handling practices, demonstrate evidence of experience working with artworks in a museum or 
public art setting, and follow conservation recommendations provided by Metro Arts.” 
 
2. Amend clause 4.1 Contract Value to increase the contract by $70,000.00 for a total of 
$270,000.00. The revised clause shall read as follows: 
 
“This Contract has an estimated value of $270,000.00. The pricing details are included in  
Exhibit A and are made a part of this Contract by reference. CONTRACTOR shall be paid as work 
is completed and METRO is, accordingly, invoiced” 
 
This amendment shall not be binding upon the parties until it has been signed by the CONTRACTOR and 
authorized representatives of the Metropolitan Government and filed in the office of the Metropolitan 
Clerk.
