AMENDMENT 2 TO THE AMENDED AND RESTATED FUEL PURCHASE CONTRACT 
BETWEEN THE 
METROPOLITAN GOVERNMENT OF NASHVILLE AND DAVIDSON COUNTY 
AND CONSTELLATION NEWENERGY, INC. 
WHEREAS, the Metropolitan Government of Nashville and Davidson County ("Metro") and 
Constellation NewEnergy, Inc. ("Constellation") are parties to an agreement (the 
1'Contract") dated 
on or about December 6, 2005, under which Constellation furnishes fuel to the Nashville District 
Energy System; and 
WHEREAS, the parties propose to amend the Contract as set forth herein. 
NOW, THEREFORE, THE PARTIES AGREE AS FOLLOWS: 
I. 
All of the terms of the Contract not specifically modified herein shall remain in full 
force and effect. 
2. 
This Amendment will become effective (the Amendment Effective Date) upon the 
first day of the month after it has been fully executed and filed with the 
Metropolitan Clerk. The term of the Contract is hereby renewed and extended (the 
"Second Renewal Term") for sixty (60) months, beginning on July 1, 2020. This 
Amendment does not preclude the possibility of further renewals and extensions as 
provided by the Contract. 
3. 
During the Second Renewal Term, the Metropolitan Government shall have the 
right to terminate the Agreement upon six (6) months' written notice to 
Constellation. 
4. 
Beginning on the Amendment Effective Date, the Fuel Management Fee shall be 
$4,950.00 per calendar month, escalated beginning July 1, 2021 , using the same 
formula used in the Management Contract to adjust the FOC.
