NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants,

successors and assigns, as follows:

SECTION 1. TERM - the term of this Agreement shall be for a
period of one (1) year, commencing on the effective date of this
Agreement and shall be automatically renewed on a year-to-year
basis. Provided however, this Agreement may be terminated by
either party by giving written notice to the other party at least
thirty (30) days prior to any annual renewal date hereof.

SECTION 2. OPERATING RIGHTS - Airline is permitted to use,
in common with others authorized so to do, the Triturator
Facilities of Nashville International Airport for the disposal of
waste material required in connection with support activities for
a Scheduled Airline Business. .

SECTION 3. OPERATING RESPONSIBILITIES OF AUTHORITY - The
Authority will manage and administer the Triturator Facilities in
support of Airline Operations at the Airport, and shall be
responsible for the following:

1. Construction of facilities and installation of all

. equipment.

2. Billing each Airline monthly a fee for debt service and
Operation & Maintenance Costs identified with the
operation of the Triturator Facility based upon the
Airline’s previous months total of enplaned passengers.

3. Maintaining the facilities in a clean, neat and
sanitary condition.

4. Repairing and maintaining the equipment and fixtures
associated with the operation of the Triturator
Facilities.

5. Providing for sanitary trash removal.

6. Providing sweeping, snow removal and roadway
maintenance surrounding the facility.

SECTION 4. PRIVILEGES AND OBLIGATIONS OF AIRLINE - As on

Airline Tenant at the Nashville International Airport and a user
of the Triturator Facility, Airline will be responsible for the
following:

1. Pumping the waste material from Airline Equipment into
Triturator Facility.

SECTION 5. OPERATING FACILITIES - For the disposal of
wastes, Airline shall use the area on the Airport suitable for
such activities as shall be designated by the Authority. The use
of such facilities shall be subject to such specific rules and
modifications as the Authority may require as the exigencies of
the immediate situation may dictate for the safe and proper
handling of vehicular traffic transporting such waste on the
apron area.

SECTION 6. USER FEES - Airline shall pay to the Authority
as compensation for construction and use of the Triturator
Facility the following:

1. A per enplanement fee established by the Authority to

cover the construction cost, debt service and
Operation and Maintenance Costs identified with the
operation of the Triturator Facility, as agreed in the
approved MII Ballot titled "Construct Triturator/Wash
Rack"

These user fees may be adjusted from time to time to
reflect as closely as possible, the actual debt service and
Operation & Maintenance Costs identified with the operation of
the Triturator Facility.
