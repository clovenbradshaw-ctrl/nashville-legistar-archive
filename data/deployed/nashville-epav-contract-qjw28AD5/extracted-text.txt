This Contract (“Contract” or “Agreement”) is entered into this 9th day of July, 2022, by 
and between the Metropolitan Government of Nashville and Davidson County, Tennessee, acting 
by and through the authority delegated to The Nashville Fair by the Metropolitan Board of Fair 
Commissioners (collectively, “METRO”), and Family American Magical Entertainment, Inc, an 
individual (state of formation and entity type, or individual), d/b/a/ World of Wonders (if 
applicable), with a business address of P.O. Box 907, Gibsonton, FL 33534 (“EVENT”). 
 
METRO operates The Nashville Fair, which shall be held on September 9-18, 2022 on the 
premises of The Fairgrounds Nashville (“Fair Premises”).  EVENT provides entertainment as 
described herein and enters into this Contract in order to provide such entertainment at The 
Nashville Fair (“Performance”).  In consideration of the mutual covenants, terms and conditions 
set forth herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which 
are hereby acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows.  In the event of a conflict between this 
Agreement and any other Exhibit or Attachment, the terms of this Agreement shall control.  
 
1. Services. 
 
 The EVENT agrees to provide the entertainment described in the attached rider, 
Exhibit A. 
 The EVENT also agrees to provide the additional labor, equipment, and other 
necessary items and services as described in the attached rider, Exhibit B. 
 METRO agrees to provide the facilities, equipment, and other items as described 
in the attached rider, Exhibit C.

2. 
Term.  
The Contract shall become effective upon the Contract being fully executed 
by all Parties.  The Term of the Contract and shall end on September 18, 2022, the last day of The 
Nashville Fair.   
3. 
 Compensation. 
METRO agrees to pay $16,000.00 to EVENT for the entertainment 
provided in accordance with the terms and conditions of this Contract.  This compensation shall 
not exceed $16,000.00.   
EVENT shall be paid after their performance, upon determination by METRO that EVENT has 
satisfactorily provided the agreed upon EVENT services.  There will be no other charges or fees 
for the performance of this Contract.  Payment shall be by check.   
 
4. 
Termination.  
Should EVENT fail to fulfill its obligations under the Contract in a 
timely and proper manner, or if it should violate any term of the Contract, METRO shall have the 
right to immediately terminate the Contract.  Such termination shall not relieve EVENT of any 
liability to METRO for damages sustained by virtue of any breach by EVENT.  Should funding 
for this Contract be discontinued, METRO shall have the right to terminate this Contract 
immediately upon written notice to EVENT.  METRO may also terminate this Contract at any 
time upon thirty (30) days written notice to EVENT.   
 
5. 
Recording and Broadcast Rights. EVENT agrees to allow METRO to photograph, film 
and/or record EVENT’s activities at The Nashville Fair and permits the limited use of such 
photographs or recordings, including publication, reproduction, and distribution, by METRO 
solely for promotional and fundraising purposes associated with The Nashville Fair.   
 
6. 
Parking.  METRO shall provide two (2) parking spaces for EVENT, which shall be in 
designated parking spaces on the Fair Premises.  METRO shall not be responsible for any loss or 
damage occurring to vehicles brought onto Fair Premises by EVENT, its employees and 
subcontractors.  
 
7. 
Access. 
EVENT shall have right of access to the premises as permitted by METRO, 
as well as access to the Fair Premises as necessary for the purposes of installing and removing 
necessary goods and equipment.  EVENT, its subcontractors, and employees shall have access to 
METRO premises upon the presentation of passes to be issued by METRO.  METRO reserves the 
right to exclude or eject all objectionable persons from the Fair Premises without liability. 
 
8. 
Control of Premises.  METRO maintains the right to control the management and 
operation of the Fair Premises and enforce the necessary and proper rules of METRO and The 
Nashville Fair at all times.   
 
9. 
Condition and Maintenance of Facility.  EVENT shall maintain the Fair Premises that it 
occupies during the course of its Performance in a safe and orderly manner and cause all debris to 
be properly discarded.  EVENT shall immediately pay METRO for the cost of repairing any 
damage to the Fair Premises caused by the acts, omissions, or negligence of EVENT, its employees 
and contractors, or its invitees during the term of this Contract.

Responsibility for Property.  METRO assumes no responsibility for any property placed 
or left on the Fair Premises by EVENT and is hereby expressly relieved and discharged from any

11. 
Lost or Abandoned Property.  METRO shall have the right to collect and maintain 
custody of all items left on the Fair Premises after the expiration of the term of the Contract.  Any 
property left on the Fair Premises after that point shall be deemed abandoned by EVENT and may 
be disposed of by METRO as its sole discretion.  EVENT shall be responsible for any costs 
involved with the storage, disposal, or removal of its abandoned property. 
 
12. 
Hazardous Substances and Weapons.  EVENT represents and warrants that it shall not 
cause or permit any hazardous substances, including fire hazards, or illegal weapons, or other 
potentially dangerous objects to be brought into METRO premises.   
 
13. 
Conduct. 
EVENT shall not willfully commit any act or do anything which might 
reasonably be considered: i) to be immoral, deceptive, scandalous or obscene, or ii) to injure, 
tarnish, damage or otherwise negatively affect the community and/or the reputation and goodwill 
associated with METRO and The Nashville Fair.   In the event METRO determines that EVENT 
has violated this clause, METRO may, upon written notice to EVENT, immediately suspend or 
terminate this Agreement.
