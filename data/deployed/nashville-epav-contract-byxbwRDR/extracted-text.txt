IX. 
Maintenance: Repairs and Restoration 
A. The Artwork must be durable, taking into consideration that the Site is an 
unsecured public space that may be exposed to elements such as weather,

equipment.  ARTIST must ensure that the Artwork’s maintenance 
requirements will be reasonable in terms of time and expense. 
B. METRO and not ARTIST will provide for the maintenance of the Artwork.

limited and determined in significant part by funding decisions of the 
Metropolitan Council and other officials and departments.

integrity of the Artwork, such as the application of protective or anti-graffiti 
coatings.  
D. The ARTIST warrants that foreseeable exposure to the elements and 
general wear and tear will not cause the Artwork to fall below an acceptable 
standard of public display.  
E. Prior to receipt of fourth payment, ARTIST shall provide a maintenance 
manual with a description of all materials, products and fabrication methods

product data sheets for any material or finish used; (ii) the names and 
contact information of relevant manufacturers or producers; and, (iii) to the

warranty, ARTIST shall provide copies of such warranties. The ARTIST 
warrants that reasonable maintenance of the Artwork will not require 
procedures substantially in excess of those described in the recommended 
maintenance manual.

EXHIBIT E 
Design Proposal  
 
Site 
The artwork will be located in the interior of the Bellevue Branch Library. Titled Rise 
Above, the first part will be suspended above the circulation desk and tech commons 
area near the clerestory windows. The second part is a mobile suspended over the 
Toddler and Infant Interactive Space with hanging leaves and one bird. 
 
 
 
 
 
Artwork Description 
 
Soaring Birds: A long flowing line of birds in the vaulted 90’ area beginning at the back of 
the building (16’ wide) decreasing in volume and size toward the entrance and above the 
circulation desk. The abstract birds will be simple in form, the largest wing span of 3’. A 
variety of sizes fly together and varying heights, but in uniform elevation, all decreasing 
in size as they approach circulation. The birds are made from the hackberry trees 
salvaged from the construction site. They will be natural in color plus 2 soft wood tone 
shades and an occasional color picked up from the designer’s palette.
