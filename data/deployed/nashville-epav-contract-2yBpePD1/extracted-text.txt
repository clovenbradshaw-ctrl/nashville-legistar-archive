RECITALS 
WHEREAS, the State and Metro entered into a certain Lease Agreement dated May 18, 
1995 (the "Original Lease"); 
WHEREAS, the State and Metro mutually acknowledge and agree that the Original 
Lease is currently in effect and that neither of the Parties are in breach or default of the Original 
Lease; 
WHEREAS, the north end of the Farmers' Market (the "NorthTract"), which is more 
particularly described in Schedule 1 and incorporated into this Amendment Number Two, is 
included in the Leased Premises; 
WHEREAS, the State and Metro mutually agree to amend the Original Lease as set forth 
below. 
NOW, THEREFORE, based upon the mutual promises contained herein and other good 
and valuable consideration, the receipt of which is hereby acknowledged, the State and Metro 
hereby agree to amend and modify the Original Lease as follows: 
1. In exchange for the sum of four million dollars ($4,000,000) to be paid by the State to
Metro, Metro has agreed that the northern portion of the property ("the North Tract")
containing the North Shed facility and adjacent parking lot shall no longer be a part of
the "Leased Premise" or subject to the terms and conditions of the Original Lease or
any amendment thereto. Accordingly, the Original Lease is hereby amended by
excluding the property described on Schedule 1 of this Amendment from the
description of the Leased Premises found in Exhibit A of the Original Lease.
2.
Metro shall have right to ingress and egress to the North Tract at a location
designated by the State for the for purposes of loading and unloading supplies at the
north dock area.

3. Metro shall have from the date of execution of this lease amendment until April 30,
2016 to utilize the North Tract and shall assume all maintenance associated with the
property for said time period. Upon completion of the new parking lot by the State
on the North Tract, said parking lot shall be available for use by both the Farmers'
Market and the State for the duration of the Lease ( 40 years including renewals). The
State shall provide Metro with at least 15 days prior written notice of an event which
will require the State's exclusive use of the parking lot for events and for a specified
period of time.
4. The State is not bound by this Amendment Number Two until it is signed by the
Parties and approved by appropriate officials in accordance with applicable
Tennessee laws and regulations.
5. This Agreement shall not be effective until the terms of this Amendment have been
approved by a resolution of the Metropolitan Council and authorized by the
Metropolitan Mayor.
6.
The revisions set forth herein shall be effective as of the date this Amendment
Number Two is fully executed. All other terms and conditions of the Original Lease
not expressly amended herein shall remain in full force and effect.
7.
This instrument may be executed in one or more counterparts. It shall be fully
executed when each Party whose signature is required has signed at least one (1)
counterpart, even though no one (1) counterpart contains the signatures of all parties
to this instrument. Electronic, scanned or facsimile signatures shall have the same
force and effect as original signatures. All capitalized terms used but not defined in
this Amendment Number Two shall have the meanings ascribed to them in the
Original Lease.
