AMENDMENT NUMBER 1 TO GRANT #AA2001 
BETWEEN 
THE METROPOLITAN GOVERNMENT OF NASHVILLE AND DAVIDSON COUNTY  
AND  
ACTORS BRIDGE ENSEMBLE THEATER OF NASHVILLE, INC. 
 
This Amendment is entered into on the day this document is filed with the Metropolitan Clerk’s 
Office by and between THE METROPOLITAN GOVERNMENT OF NASHVILLE AND 
DAVIDSON COUNTY (METRO) and ACTORS BRIDGE ENSEMBLE THEATER OF 
NASHVILLE, INC., located in Nashville, TN.  
 
WITNESSETH 
 
WHEREAS, the parties desire to modify the terms and conditions and to add or delete certain 
other terms and conditions to their original agreement dated 9/25/2019, Metro Grant numbered 
A-40872, hereinafter the “GRANT”, the parties hereby agree: 
 
This amendment effects the following changes to the contract. 
 
1. Section A.1 under the Scope of Services is deleted in its entirety, and the following 
language is substituted: 
 
The Grantee shall employ the funds made available under this grant in accordance with the program 
submitted in the attached Exhibit ____ for a Basic Operating Support grant and subject to the Arts 
Commission’s Grant Guidelines and to the terms and conditions set forth in Contract #AA2001. 
 
2. Exhibit ___ shall be included as part of the Grant Contract. 
 
3. Upon filing of the Amendment with the Metro Clerk, the amended Scope of Services 
shall be deemed effective as to the entire term of the Grant as identified in B.1.   
 
This Amendment shall not increase Metro’s maximum liability as described in C.1. Except as 
modified by the Amendment, all terms and conditions set forth in Grant #AA2001 shall remain 
in full force and effect.
