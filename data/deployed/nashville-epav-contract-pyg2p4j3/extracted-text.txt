3 .1. 
INSULATION SHALL BE APPLIED ONLY AFTER PIPE OR EQUIPMENT HAS BEEN 
HYDROSTATICALLY OR OTHERWISE TESTED AND PROVEN "SERVICE READY." 
INSULATION SHALL NOT BE APPLIED TO FLANGES PRIOR TO RE-TIGHTENING 
AFTER INITIAL OPERATION. ALL SURFACES WILL BE CLEANED OF DIRT, OIL, 
GREASE AND OTHER FOREIGN MATTER PRIOR TO THE INSTALLATION OF THE 
INSULATION MATERIAL. 
3 .2. 
INSULATION APPLIED TO BENDS, FITTINGS AND IRREGULARLY SHAPED PIPING 
SHALL BE PREFORMED FITTING INSULATION MATCHJNG PIPELINE INSULATION 
IN THICKNESS AND MATERIAL. WHERE PREFORMED FITTING INSULATION IS NOT 
AVAILABLE BECAUSE OF SIZE, TYPE OR IRREGULAR FITTING OR PIPELINE 
SURF ACE, PIPELINE INSULATION MATERIAL SHALL BE MITERED INTO LENGTHS 
THAT BUTT TIGHTLY AND SECURELY TOGETHER TO COVER AND FOLLOW THE 
PIPING SURFACE AND SHALL MATCH THJCKNESS OF THE ADJACENT PIPELINE 
INSULATION. THE INSULATION SHALL BE SECURELY WIRED INTO PLACE AT 
INTERVALS OF NOT MORE THAN 15° OF AN ARC. INSULATING CEMENT SHALL BE 
USED TO FILL JOINTS AND VOIDS IN THE FABRICATED INSULATION AND 
FINISHED TO PROVIDE A SMOOTH SURF ACE. A SURF ACE COAT OF SEALING 
MASTIC, REINFORCED WITH INSULATING CLOTH, SHALL FINISH THE FITTING 
COVER AND SEAL IT TO THE ADJACENT PIPELINE JACKET. THE MANUFACTURER'S 
INSTRUCTIONS SHALL BE FOLLOWED FOR INSTALLING OVERSIZED INSULATION 
AT ELBOWS WHERE PIPE MOVEMENT IS ANTICIPATED. 
3.3. 
IN ALL CASES AND PIPING SYSTEM CONFIGURATIONS, THE INTERFACE 
TEMPERATURE BETWEEN LAYERS OF INSULATION OR INSULATING JACKET 
SHALL NOT EXCEED THE ALLOWABLE WORKING TEMPERATURE OF THE 
INSULATING OR JACKET MATERIALS BASED ON A 90°F AMBIENT TEMPERATURE 
AND A SURFACE AIR VELOCITY OF ZERO (0) FEET PER MINUTE. 
3.4. 
WHERE NEW PIPING IS CONNECTED TO EXISTING PIPING HAVING A DIFFERENT 
INSULATION THICKNESS THAN WHAT IS SPECIFIED UNDER THIS SECTION, THE 
GREATER THJCKNESS OF INSULATION (EXISTING OR THIS SPECIFICATION) SHALL 
BE USED. TRANSITIONS IN INSULATION THICKNESS SHALL BE MADE WITH 
TAPERED SECTIONS OF INSULATION AND INSULATED CEMENT TO FORM A 
SMOOTH, TAPERED CHANGE IN THICKNESS, FABRICATED AS SPECIFIED FOR 
FIELD-APPLIED FITTING INSULATION PER SECTION 3 .2.

INSULATION THAT IS DAMP, DISCOLORED, DAMAGED OR DOES NOT FIT THE 
PIPING PROPERLY SHALL NOT BE USED. ALL ADHESIVES, CEMENTS AND MASTICS

the percentage of condensate returned to the EGF during that period and the average temperature 
of that condensate from instrumentation readings of the condensate return. Then, the guaranteed 
maximum gas usage rate will be found in Table D-1 by looking at the cell in the table for that 
temperature (column) and percentage return (row). For values of percentage return and/or return 
temperature in between the values listed in Table D-1, the maximum gas usage rate will be 
determined by linear interpolation. The resulting value for the maximum gas usage rate will be 
used as Qo from Section 7.12.
