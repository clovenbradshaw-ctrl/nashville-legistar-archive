THIRD AMENDMENT TO LEASE AGREEMENT METROPOLITAN
MILLER TRANSFER & RIGGING COMPANY CLERK

THIS THIRD AMENDMENT TO LEASE AGREEMENT, made and entered into this
64h day of February , 2007, to be effective as of the as of the 1st day of April, 2007, by
and between the METROPOLITAN NASHVILLE AIRPORT AUTHORITY, a public corporation
existing under the laws of the State of Tennessee, hereinafter referred to as "Authority", and
MILLER TRANSFER & RIGGING CO., a corporation authorized to do business in the State of
Tennessee, hereinafter referred to as "Lessee".

WITNESSETH:

WHEREAS, Authority and Lessee entered into a Lease Agreement dated May 23, 2000,
("the Agreement"), granting Lessee the right to lease certain improved property at the Nashville
International Airport (“the Airport”) for Lessee’s operation of an air cargo freight forwarding
service company at the Airport; and,

WHEREAS, Authority and Lessee desire to amend the Lease Agreement by permitting the
Lease to be additionally extended for nine (1) year periods.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the premises, mutual covenants, terms and
conditions contained herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and
sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Agreement is hereby amended in the following
respect only:

1. Article II. TERM, Section 2.1, is hereby amended by substituting the third sentence

of Section 2.1, with the following;

Thereafter, this Lease Agreement may be extended for Nine (9) additional one (1)
year periods and shall be deemed to be automatically exercised unless either party
receives written notice from the other of their intent not to renew this Agreement not


less than ninety (90) days prior to the expiration of the basic term or renewal option.
Either party may terminate this agreement, at any time for any reason, with sixty (60)
days advance written notice.”
In the event of any conflict, inconsistency or incongruity between the provisions of this
Third Amendment and any provisions of the Agreement, the more restrictive on Lessee shall in all
respects govern and control.
The parties expressly agree that unless specifically modified herein, all other terms,
conditions and provisions of the Agreement remain in full force and effect.


IN WITNESS WHEREOPF, the parties hereto have caused this instrument to be executed
by their duly authorized representatives on the day and date first above written.
