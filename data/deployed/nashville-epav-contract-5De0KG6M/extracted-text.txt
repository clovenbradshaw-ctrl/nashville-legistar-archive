
1.
References

The“Project”meansDesignBuildforFarmers'MarketGrandEntranceandWasteRelocation.

“Owner”and“Metro”meantheMetropolitanGovernmentofNashvilleandDavidsonCounty.

“Contractor”meansDowdleConstructionGroup,LLC.,theDesignBuildContractor.“Design
Team”meansallmembersofContractor’sdesignteam,includingarchitects,engineers,and
consultants.TheDesignTeamshallbeundercontractwithContractor.Contractoris
responsibleforprovidingalldesignandconstructionnecessaryforacompleteandproperly
functioningProjectona“turnkey”basis.The“Work”orthe“ProjectWork”meansthedesign,
constructionandallrelatedfunctionsandservicesrequiredtocompletelyfulfillContractor’s
obligationsunderthisAgreement.TheFixedPriceforConstructionor“FPC”isthepricetobe
paidbyMetroforsatisfactoryconstructionoftheProject,tobeestablishedbyamendmentto
thisAgreementassetforthherein.Metro’sdesiredcompletiondatefortheProjectis:March,
29,2019.
