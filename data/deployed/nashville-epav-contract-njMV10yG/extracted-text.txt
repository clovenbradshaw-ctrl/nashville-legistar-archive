Design Proposal  
September 5, 2018 
This letter is express my interest in creating a series of artworks for the Nashville 
Farmers Market. Through my artistic practice, I aspire to create environmental artwork that 
honors and inspires communities. My mission is to create art that can be a source of community 
pride, and a tool for social and cultural development. Typically, my designs are informed by and 
recall elements of the social and natural context of a site and its surrounding communities.  
As a part of the 2018 outdoor exhibit Building Better Tables, I collaborated with Nella 
Pearl Frieserson, founder of the Brooklyn Heights Community Garden to create Biscuits and 
Braids event held on August 11 in the Haynes neighborhood. In my Frogtown neighborhood I’ve 
worked with neighbors to create an outdoor kitchen on Frogtown Farm that we envisioned as the 
Hearth of the Farm and heart of the neighborhood. Working with an innovate outdoor oven called 
the “Relic” designed in Nashville, I baked over 100 biscuits served to girls, who were getting their 
heads braided and the women who were braiding their hair in an annual back to school event. 
The event served to bring residents together to literally and figuratively “break bread”. Working 
together we can use bread to not only bring a neighborhood together, but help increase food 
equity. 
My artwork investigates the integration of art and nature. Over the last ten years I’ve 
pursued an artistic, academic and community course of study to blend the two. My research into 
the relationship of plants and humans, water, agriculture, and environmental theory, has 
increased my understanding of the natural environment. Those who experience my artwork will 
hopefully be informed and inspired to create their own interaction with nature. These practices 
also will serve to create art for the Nashville Farmers Market. If selected to create a series of 
artworks for the Nashville Farmers Market, I will bring my critical understanding of food, nature, 
plants and my experiences as an artist to create artwork that informs and inspires. 
Sincerely, 
Seitu Ken Jones
