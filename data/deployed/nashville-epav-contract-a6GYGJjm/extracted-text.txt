2. RENT, RATES, AND PAYMENTS.
a. Venue Rental Fee. $ 14,400.00
b. Additional Event-Related Fees. In addition to the Venue Rental Fee, the Licensee agrees to pay 
the Licensor the sum of equipment and ancillary rentals plus taxes for the use of the premises. 
Event-Related Fees shall be equal to the total of fees for furniture, fixture, and equipment rentals, 
utility hookups, internet and data connections, technology fees, labor, administrative fees for 
securing cleaning services and other services, and support personnel provided by Licensor for the 
Event. Event-Related Fee Estimate: $ 3,800.00
c. General Deposits. One-Half (½) of the total amount due is required as a non-refundable deposit 
within 14-days of signing this license agreement by both parties.  Licensor will provide Licensee an 
invoice of the amount due.
d. Payments. The entirety of the Venue Rental Fee balance and estimated ancillary rentals is due no 
less than 14-days prior to move-in.
e. Forms of Payment. Only credit cards, business checks, bank cashier’s checks, or money orders 
are accepted. Credit cards are subject to and Licensee shall pay any fees charged by credit card 
processors. No personal checks are accepted.
f. Final Invoice. Any account balance including charges incurred during the License Period will be 
due upon receipt of final invoice. Balances not paid within 30 days of the end of the License Period 
shall be subject to interest at the highest rate permitted by law from the date of the final bill, and 
such interest shall be due and payable upon payment of the final bill. Such late fees are in addition 
to any other rights and remedies available to Licensor.
g. Taxes. Licensee is responsible for all applicable local, State, and Federal taxes. Tax exempt 
organizations must complete and file with the Licensor all applicable tax-exempt forms at least 30-
days prior to License Period.
3. OPERATIONS: AGREEMENTS, COVENANTS, AND RESTRICTIONS.
a. Control. The Fairgrounds reserves the right throughout the Term to control the operation and 
management of the premises. The Fairgrounds Executive Director and staff have the right to enter 
the premises at any time for any purpose, including removal of any person who, in the sole 
judgment, of the Executive Director or designated assistants, is disrupting or obstructing the proper 
operation and management of the premises.
b. Use of Premises. The Executive Director and staff may use any part of the premises during Move-
In, during Move-Out, and at any time Licensee is not actively using the particular part of the 
premises, so long as the Executive Director’s use does not interfere with the Licensee’s use.
c. Utilities. The Fairgrounds will provide customary lighting, heating, air conditioning, and water at no 
additional cost to the Licensee. If typical utilities become unavailable, Licensor shall have the right 
to approve any alternative utility arrangement and shall charge for using electric outlets. Licensor 
may provide special utility services requested by Licensee at additional fees.
d. Building Layout. The Licensee must submit a complete building diagram of the event setup to 
The Fairgrounds Office Staff at least 30 days prior to the event. Failure to comply with this 
requirement may result in closure of the show or delayed opening of the event. The Licensee will 
assume full responsibility for obtaining Metro Fire Marshal approval on diagrams not received in 
Fairgrounds’ office 30 days prior to the event.
e. Exclusive and Specialized Services. Licensor retains the exclusive right to provide all Food and 
Beverage services. No outside food and beverages may be brought into the Licensed Premises 
without the written approval of the Licensor. Licensor reserves the right to approve specialized 
services employed by the Licensee including, but not limited to, decorators, rigging, and audio/
visual providers.
f. Signage. Licensor reserves the right to approve all signage plans. Licensee must submit signage 
requests to the Licensee at least 30 days prior to the event.
g. Security.  At least one Tennessee Highway Patrol Trooper will be at the event to provide security 
and to maintain law and order.

days.

i.
Licensee will be charged a penalty not less than 25% of the Base Rental Fee per additional 
hour per building or facility if the event end times are not adhered to or the facilities are not 
vacated of all persons by the time specified in section 1.
ii. If Licensee fails to surrender the premises to The Fairgrounds at the expiration of the term 
for use stated herein, then The Fairgrounds may remove from the premises all effects 
remaining therein and store the same wherever it sees fit at Licensee’s cost, expense, and 
risk. The Fairgrounds shall not be liable to Licensee for removing and storing such effects. 
For such additional periods as any effects of Licensee remain at The Fairgrounds, Licensee 
shall pay The Fairgrounds double the rate for the space. In addition, Licensee shall be liable 
to The Fairgrounds for any claim or damages suffered by The Fairgrounds resulting from 
Licensee’s failure to surrender the premises to The Fairgrounds in as good condition as 
Licensee received the same.
