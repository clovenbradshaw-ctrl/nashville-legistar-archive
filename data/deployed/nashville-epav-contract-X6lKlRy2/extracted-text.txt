This Grant Contract, by and between the Metropolitan Government of Nashville and Davidson County, Tennessee, by and through the 
Metropolitan Nashville Arts Commission, hereinafter referred to as the "Grantor" and Boys and Girls Club of Middle 
Tennessee, Inc., hereinafter referred to as the "Grantee," is for application number 16Ml9 for the provision of a/an Arts 
Access Project Support grant, as further defined in the "SCOPE OF SERVICES." 
The Grantee is a nonprofit charitable or civic organization. The Grantee's address is listed below in E.2. The Grantee's application is 
on file with the Gran tor in the Grantee's file for application number 16M 19. 
The Grantee's place of incorporation or organization is the State of Tennessee. 
A.
SCOPE OF SERVICES:
A. I.
The Grantee shall employ the funds made available under this grant in accordance with the project/program submitted in the
application for a/an Arts Access Project Support grant, which application is incorporated herein, and subject to the Arts
Commission's FY2016 Grant Guidelines and to the terms and conditions set forth herein.
A.2.
The Grantee, under this Grant Contract, will spend funds solely for the purpose set forth in application number 16AA I 9.
B.
GRANT CONTRACT TERM:
8.1. 
Grant Contract Tenn. This Grant Contract shall be effective for the period commencing on July I, 20 I 5 and ending on June
I, 2016. The Grantor shall have no obligation for services rendered by the Grantee which are not perfonned within the
specified period.
C.
PAYMENT TERMS AND CONDITIONS:
C. I.
Maximum Liability. In no event shall the maximum liability of the Grantor under this Grant Contract exceed $3, 175. The
Grantee agrees to match with cash the Grantor's compensation under this grant with a minimum sum of$3,175 as identified
in application number I 6AA 19. The Grantee shall use the award and match in the manner provided on the approved Revised
Budget, attached and incorporated herein as a part of this Grant Contract as Attachment One (I). The Revised Budget line­
items include, but are not limited to, all applicable taxes, fees, overhead, and all other direct and indirect costs incurred or to
be incurred by the Grantee.
C.2.
Compensation Finn. The maximum liability of the Grantor under this Grant Contract is firm for the duration of the Grant
Contract and is not subject to escalation for any reason unless amended pursuant to Section D.2 of this Grant Contract.
C .3. 
Payment Methodology. Upon execution of the Grant Contract and receipt of a request for payment, the Grantee may be
eligible to receive up to eighty percent (80%) of the grant amount. The final payment, or remaining twenty percent (20%) of
the grant amount, will be made only after the Grantee has completed the project/program and properly documented all
monies spent (including the grantee's match) toward the project/program. The Grantee shall expend these grant funds and
the required match before requesting additional grant funds, in the manner described in C.3.a.
C.3.a. 
The final Grant Contract payment shall be made only after the Grantee has completed the project/program and has submitted
a final financial report of expenditures documenting a full accounting of the expenditures made in accomplishing this Grant 
Contract, including its own cash match, identification and amounts of in-kind contributed services (if any). The final financial 
report shall be completed in accordance with the Revised Budget on file with the Grantor in the Grantee's file and shall be in 
fonn and substance acceptable to the Grantor. 
C.3.b. 
The final payment request and financial report of expenditures are to be submitted by the Grantee within thirty (30) days after
the ending date of the project/program, or by June I, 2016, whichever comes first. Project grantees must submit the Closeout 
Report with their final payment request. Basic Operating Support grantees must submit the Closeout Report no later than July 
31, 2016.
