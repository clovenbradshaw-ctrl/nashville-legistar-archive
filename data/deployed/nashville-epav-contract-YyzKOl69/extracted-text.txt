Business Information Systems 
333 Industrial Park Road 
Piney Flats, TN 37686 
 
Business Information Systems (BIS) has implemented the ability for on demand decal printing in the TN County Clerk 
management software. The software will securely print, track, and provide reporting for the on demand decal printing.  
 
The decal will print on thermal transfer paper and reflective sheathing stock. On demand decals will increase security at the 
State and County Clerk level since no pre-printed stock will exist. The software will print the plate number on the reflective 
sheathing and track the print job. Printing the plate number as the decal number, will eliminate fraud. 
 
The County Clerk’s productivity will increase by not having to match decal numbers to the decal numbers assigned to the 
Motor Vehicle transaction. Instead, the software will print the decal based on the Motor Vehicle transaction thus, 
eliminating the chance of a clerk stapling the wrong decal to a transaction. Since no inventory is preprinted, stock would 
have no value eliminating potential theft. The Clerk’s office will no longer be required to maintain preprinted decal 
numbers in inventory.   
 
BIS has determined that 38 thermal printers will be required for the Phase One implementation of On Demand Decal 
printing at the Davidson County Clerk’s office.  
 
The initial printers required will allow the Davidson County Clerk’s offices to begin printing decals on demand. By October 
2014, a second printer may be required at each location to allow for the issuance of concurrent years’ decals. BIS will work 
with the Davidson County Clerk’s office to determine the number of additional printers that may be required.  If needed, 
these printers will be billed separately. 
 
Upgrades to existing motor vehicle software, ‘TN Clerk’, for proper installation and configuration of required decal printing 
software and hardware include the following: 
 
Decal Printing Software 
 
Thermal Decal Printers (Quantity 38) 
 
Installation & Configuration for ‘TN Clerk’ compatibility 
 
1 year maintenance 
The estimated additional cost will be $25,000.00. 
The purchase of additional equipment and services for Decals-on-Demand will be priced accordingly. 
Software, peripheral equipment and configuration requests, to comply with future needs or operational changes required 
by the TN Department of Revenue, will be priced accordingly.
