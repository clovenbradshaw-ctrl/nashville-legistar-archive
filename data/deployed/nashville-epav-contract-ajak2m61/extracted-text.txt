This agreement (“Agreement”) is entered into this 28th day of April, 2026, by and between the 
Metropolitan Government of Nashville and Davidson County, by and through the authority 
delegated to the Farmers’ Market Board and its Executive Director (“LICENSOR”), and  Radical 
Shoots, LLC , an Tennessee LLC), d/b/a Radical Shoots (if applicable) (“LICENSEE”).   
 
LICENSOR owns and operates the Nashville Farmers’ Market (“Farmers’ Market”), located at 
900 Rosa L. Parks Blvd., Nashville, Tennessee 37208, including freezer and cooler space located 
in the basement of the Market House building (“Freezer Premises”).   LICENSEE is a current 
approved vendor or tenant or is applying to become a vendor or tenant at the Farmers’ Market and 
wishes to obtain a license to use and access space in the freezers and coolers.   Under the terms 
and conditions set forth below, the Parties agree as follows: 
 
1. License. LICENSOR agrees to  reserve space for LICENSEE in the freezer(s) or cooler(s) 
designated as follows: Market House Cooler #4. 
LICENSOR grants LICENSEE a limited and revocable license to use and access such space 
for the purpose of storing perishable items to be used or sold in the course of LICENSEE’s 
operations at the Farmers’ Market.   The Farmers’ Market reserves the right, at its sole 
discretion, to assign an alternative freezer or cooler space to LICENSEE when doing so is in 
the best interest of the Farmers’ Market’s operations or public safety. 
2. Term. 
The Term of this Agreement shall begin on the 1st day of May, 2026, and end on 
the 28th day of  February, 2027.  This license is renewable for a 12-month term, pending review 
and approval of the Farmers’ Market staff.

3. Fee. 
LICENSEE agrees to pay the following sum, which shall be due and payable 
monthly, on or before the fifth (5th) day of each calendar month: $200.00 for Cooler #4.   
LICENSOR is not obligated to invoice LICENSEE for such fee.  In the event that LICENSEE 
occupies the Premises for a period less than a month, pro rata charges may be applicable.  Late 
payments will be subject to a penalty of $25.00 for each month and shall be due immediately, 
along with any associated payment.   
In the event LICENSEE becomes indebted to LICENSOR at any time during this Agreement, 
LICENSEE grants LICENSOR a lien on all property of LICENSEE situated on or in the 
Freezer Premises or LICENSOR premises and acknowledges that LICENSOR may take 
possession of and sell such property in order to pay any LICENSEE debt. 
 
4. Use of Premises.  
 
a) LICENSEE shall have the right to access the Freezer Premises and the specific freezer or cooler 
assigned to it by LICENSOR, including the right to enter the Market House building and 
Farmers’ Market premises for this purpose during normal business hours. LICENSEE may be 
given access to the Freezer Premises after hours at the discretion of the LICENSOR staff, 
provided that arrangements are made in advance as required by LICENSOR’s rules and 
policies. 
b) LICENSEE use shall include a revocable license to use the Common Areas, as may be 
designated from time to time by LICENSOR, subject however to these terms and conditions 
and to rules, guidelines, and facility-use policies as prescribed by LICENSOR.  The term 
“Common Areas” shall mean all Nashville Farmers’ Market space and facilities furnished and 
designated by LICENSOR for the general use, in common, by visitors and customers or renters, 
including LICENSEE, its officers, agents, employees.  Said space and facilities may include, 
but are not limited to, the parking areas, streets, passenger vehicle roadways, sidewalks, 
walkways, service areas, roadways, loading platforms, drainage and plumbing systems, roof, 
canopies, ramps, landscaped areas and other similar facilities available for common use which 
may from time to time exist.   
c) The delivery of items to and from the Freezer Premises shall be subject to LICENSOR’s 
policies. All loading and unloading shall be done only at such times, in the areas, and through 
the entrances designated by LICENSOR.  Non-compliance with LICENSOR’s policies 
regarding deliveries may result in charges of fifty dollars ($50.00) per infraction. 
d) LICENSEE shall leave the Freezer Premises in a neat and orderly condition at the end of each 
day, keep any garbage or other discarded items in containers as specified by the LICENSOR, 
and dispose of such items in designated refuse collection areas.   
e) LICENSEE may not post signs, install fixtures, or otherwise make changes to the physical 
appearance of the individual freezers or coolers or the Freezer Premises.  
f) LICENSEE equipment, merchandise, or other items of any time must be kept within 
LICENSEE’s designed freezer or cooler space.  Any items placed outside of such space may 
be subject to removal without notice at the cost of LICENSEE.

LICENSEE accepts the condition of the 
Freezer Premises as is and agrees to return the freezer and cooler spaces in the same condition

6. Farmers’ Market Rules and Policies. LICENSEE agrees to comply with all Nashville 
Farmers’ Market rules and policies, including, where applicable, the Vendor Policy Handbook 
and any Market House rules, policies, or handbook.  Failure to observe such rules and policies 
shall be considered a breach of this Agreement.   
 
7. Reservation of Rights. LICENSOR reserves the right to access the Freezer Premises, 
including all designated freezer and cooler spaces, at all times, including but not exclusively 
for purposes related to cleaning, maintenance, and health and safety.  LICENSOR will perform 
such work in the Premises in a manner that shall not unreasonably interfere with the normal 
business operations of LICENSEE.  LICENSOR reserves the right to control the management 
and operation of LICENSOR premises and enforce the necessary and proper rules and policies 
at all times. 
 
8. Violations. 
Violations of any of these provisions or of any other LICENSOR rules and 
policies may subject LICENSEE to fines of $50.00 per day or termination of this Agreement.   
 
9. Termination. 
The following shall constitute grounds for termination for default: 1) 
LICENSEE breach of material terms of conditions of this Agreement; 2) LICENSEE failure 
to abide by applicable laws; 3 LICENSEE violation of LICENSOR rules or policies.  Upon a 
determination by LICENSOR, at its sole discretion, of LICENSEE default, LICENSOR shall 
deliver written notice of such default to LICENSEE and this Agreement shall terminate.  
LICENSOR may also terminate this Agreement for convenience upon ten (10) days written 
notice.  In such circumstances LICENSOR’s liability shall be limited to credit for any unearned 
fee paid under this Agreement.  LICENSOR may also terminate this Agreement should 
LICENSOR or a superior governmental authority decide termination is necessary for a 
governmental purpose. 
 
10.  Waiver of Liability. 
LICENSOR assumes no responsibility for any LICENSEE property 
placed in or left on Freezer Premises, including any property in freezers and coolers, and is 
hereby expressly relieved and discharged from any and all liability for any loss, injury, or 
damage to LICENSEE property, including all perishable items, that may be sustained by reason 
of LICENSEE use and occupancy of Freezer Premises.  LICENSEE is solely responsible for 
all property it brings onto Freezer Premises, including all property left in Farmers’ Market 
freezers and coolers, and assumes all risk of damage or loss to such property.

11.  Abandoned Property. Any LICENSEE property left on Freezer Premises after the term of 
this Agreement shall be deemed abandoned and may be disposed of by LICENSOR at its sole 
discretion.  LICENSEE shall be responsible for any costs involved with the storage, disposal, 
or removal of its abandoned property. 
 
 
12. Licensure and Taxes. 
LICENSEE shall have all applicable licenses, permits, or similar 
documentation, and be current on its payment of all applicable taxes. 
 
13. Compliance with Laws. LICENSEE agrees to comply with all applicable local ordinances, 
including health and building codes, as well as state and federal laws and regulations. 
 
14. Insurance. 
It is recommended that LICENSEE obtain and maintain general and 
property insurance sufficient to cover all liability potentially incurred by the terms of this 
Agreement, including liability for all LICENSEE property maintained in and all LICENSEE 
conduct on the Freezer Premises.  All such insurance shall list the Metropolitan Government 
of Nashville and Davidson County as well as the State of Tennessee as additional insured 
parties. 
 
15. Indemnification.  
LICENSEE shall indemnify and hold harmless LICENSOR against 
and from any and all liabilities, obligations, damages, claims, costs, charges, expenses, and 
judgments (including, without limitation, reasonable attorney fees and expenses) arising out of 
the activities of or the use of the Premises by Licensee or Licensee’s agents, employees, 
contractors, vendors, or invitees.  LICENSEE agrees to hold LICENSOR harmless from any 
damage or loss of Licensee’s personal property located on the Premises.   LICENSEE shall 
assume any and all responsibility and liability therefore, including but not limited to costs and 
expenses incurred by LICENSOR in defense of any action. 
 
16. Attorney Fees. 
LICENSEE agrees that in the event either party takes legal action to enforce 
any provision of this Agreement or to obtain a remedy for any breach of this Agreement, and 
in the event LICENSOR prevails in such action, LICENSEE shall pay all expenses of such 
action incurred at any and all stages of the litigation, including costs, and reasonable attorney 
fees for LICENSOR. 
 
17. Joint Venture. 
This Agreement shall not in any way be construed or intended to create a 
partnership or joint venture between the Parties or to create the relationship of principal and 
agent between or among any of the Parties. None of the Parties hereto shall hold itself out in a 
manner contrary to the terms of this paragraph. No Party shall become liable for any 
representation, act, or omission of any other Party contrary to the terms of this Contract. 
 
18. Assignment. 
Neither this Agreement nor any of the rights and obligations of LICENSEE 
hereunder shall be assigned or transferred in whole or in part without the prior written consent 
of LICENSOR.  Any such assignment or transfer shall not release LICENSEE from its 
obligations under this Agreement.

20. Nondiscrimination. 
It is the policy of LICENSOR not to discriminate on the basis of 
race, creed, color, national origin, age, sex, or disability in its hiring and employment practices, 
or in admission to, access to, or operation of its programs, services, and activities.  No person 
shall be excluded from participation in, be denied benefits of, be discriminated against in the 
admission or access to, or be discriminated against in treatment or employment in 
LICENSOR’s contracted programs and activities, on grounds of race, creed, color, national 
origin, age, sex, disability, or on any other classification protected by federal or Tennessee 
State Constitutional or statutory law, nor shall they be excluded from participation in, be denied 
benefits of, or be otherwise subjected to discrimination in the performance of contracts with 
LICENSOR or in the employment practices of LICENSEE.  LICENSEE certifies and warrants 
that it will comply with this nondiscrimination requirement affirms that it will show proof of 
such nondiscrimination upon request.  
  
21. Iran Divestment Act.  In accordance with the Iran Divestment Act, Tennessee Code 
Annotated § 12-12-101 et seq., LICENSEE certifies that to the best of its knowledge and belief, 
neither LICENSEE nor any of its subcontractors are on the list created pursuant to Tennessee 
Code Annotated § 12-12-106.  Misrepresentations may result in civil and criminal sanctions, 
including contract termination, debarment, or suspension from being a contractor or 
subcontractor under LICENSOR contracts. 
 
22. Majeure. No party shall have any liability to the other hereunder by reason of any delay or 
failure to perform any obligation or covenant if the delay or failure to perform is occasioned 
by force majeure, meaning any act of God, storm, fire, casualty, pandemic or epidemic as 
reported by the World Health Organization (WHO) or Center for Disease Control and 
Prevention (CDC) or the National Institutes of Health (NIH), measures of government 
authority taken in response to a pandemic or epidemic, unanticipated work stoppage, strike, 
lockout, labor dispute, civil disturbance, riot, war, national emergency, act of Government, act 
of public enemy, or other cause of similar or dissimilar nature beyond its control. Upon a force 
majeure event, this Agreement shall terminate and LICENSEE hereby waives any claim 
against LICENSOR. 
 
23. Governing Law and Venue. 
 
The validity, construction, and effect of this 
Agreement and any and all extensions and/or modifications thereof shall be governed by the 
laws of the State of Tennessee. Tennessee law shall govern regardless of any language in any 
attachment or other document that LICENSEE may provide.  Any action between the Parties 
arising from this Agreement shall be maintained in the courts of Davidson County, Tennessee. 
 
24. Severability. 
Should any provision of this Agreement be declared to be invalid by any 
court of competent jurisdiction, such provision shall be severed and shall not affect the validity 
of the remaining provisions of this Agreement.
